# Social Media Influence Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes social media as one of the most powerful tools for building global awareness, connecting communities, mobilizing supporters and showcasing measurable social impact.

A strategic social media presence allows TJF to communicate its mission, share transformation stories, attract partnerships, engage donors and create a worldwide movement of people committed to positive change.

This Social Media Influence Strategy establishes TJF’s approach for developing a strong, authentic and influential presence across digital platforms.

The Strategy focuses on purposeful storytelling, audience engagement, community building, strategic campaigns and data-driven growth.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Build TJF’s global social media presence.
- Increase public awareness.
- Grow digital communities.
- Strengthen donor and partner engagement.
- Promote TJF programmes.
- Create long-term digital influence.
