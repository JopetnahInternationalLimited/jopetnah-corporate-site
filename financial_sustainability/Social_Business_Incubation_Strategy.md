# Social Business Incubation Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes entrepreneurship and social business development as powerful mechanisms for reducing poverty, creating employment and strengthening community resilience.

Many promising entrepreneurs and community innovators face barriers including limited access to capital, inadequate business skills, weak market connections and insufficient technical support.

This Social Business Incubation Strategy establishes TJF’s framework for identifying, nurturing and scaling impact-oriented enterprises that create economic opportunities while addressing social and environmental challenges.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s social business incubation model.
- Support entrepreneurs and innovators.
- Develop sustainable enterprises.
- Create employment opportunities.
- Promote community-based solutions.
- Build pathways to investment readiness.
