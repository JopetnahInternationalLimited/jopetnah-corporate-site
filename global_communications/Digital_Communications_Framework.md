# Digital Communications Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes digital communication as a fundamental driver of global visibility, stakeholder engagement, fundraising, partnership development and community connection.

In a digitally connected world, organizations must communicate effectively across multiple platforms while maintaining authenticity, consistency and trust.

This Digital Communications Framework establishes TJF’s approach for managing digital platforms, creating meaningful online engagement, strengthening digital communities and using technology to amplify social impact.

The Framework provides the foundation for building a professional, innovative and globally connected digital presence.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s digital communication standards.
- Strengthen online visibility.
- Improve stakeholder engagement.
- Support digital fundraising.
- Promote programme awareness.
- Build a global online community.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Official websites.
- Social media platforms.
- Email communication.
- Digital campaigns.
- Online publications.
- Multimedia content.
- Digital engagement platforms.

# 3. Digital Communication Vision

To establish TJF as a globally connected digital-first foundation that inspires action, builds trust and amplifies community impact.

TJF digital communication shall follow:

## Consistency

## Engagement

Digital platforms shall encourage dialogue and participation.

---

## Responsibility

Content shall follow ethical communication standards.

---

## Innovation

TJF shall adopt emerging digital opportunities.
