# Board Effectiveness Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that an effective Board of Directors is essential for achieving institutional excellence, maintaining accountability and protecting the Foundation’s long-term mission.

The Board serves as the guardian of TJF’s vision, values, sustainability and reputation. A high-performing Board provides strategic direction, ensures responsible governance and supports executive leadership while maintaining appropriate oversight.

This Board Effectiveness Framework establishes the principles, structures, practices and evaluation systems required for TJF’s Board to operate at international standards.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish a high-performing Board structure.
- Define Board roles and responsibilities.
- Improve strategic oversight.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Develop Board leadership capacity.
- Promote continuous Board improvement.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Board Chairperson.
- Board Directors.
- Board Committees.
- Board Advisors.
- Executive Leadership interacting with the Board.

---

# 3. Board Vision

To establish a globally respected Board of Directors that provides strategic leadership, ethical oversight and long-term institutional stewardship.

---

# 4. Board Mission

To guide TJF through effective governance, informed decision-making and responsible oversight while protecting the Foundation’s mission and values.

---

# 5. Board Effectiveness Principles

## 5.1 Strategic Leadership

The Board focuses on long-term direction rather than daily management.

---

## 5.2 Independence

Board decisions must be objective and aligned with TJF’s interests.

---

## 5.3 Diversity of Thought

The Board should include diverse:

- Skills.
- Experiences.
- Professional backgrounds.
- Regional perspectives.

---

## 5.4 Accountability

Board members are responsible for fulfilling governance duties.

---

## 5.5 Continuous Learning

The Board must continuously improve its knowledge and effectiveness.

# 8. Role of the Board Chairperson

The Chairperson shall:

- Provide Board leadership.
- Facilitate effective meetings.
- Encourage constructive discussion.
- Support accountability.
- Maintain Board effectiveness.

---

# 9. Role of Board Directors

Directors shall:

- Provide strategic guidance.
- Review institutional performance.
- Monitor risks.
- Protect TJF values.
- Support partnerships.
- Maintain confidentiality.

---

# 10. Board and Management Relationship

The Board shall:

Provide:

- Direction.
- Oversight.
- Accountability.

Management shall:

Provide:

- Implementation.
- Operational leadership.
- Performance reporting.

- Trust.
- Respect.
- Clear responsibilities.
