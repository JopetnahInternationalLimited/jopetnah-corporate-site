# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# WOMEN AND INCLUSIVE YOUTH ENTREPRENEURSHIP PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

Economic opportunity is a fundamental pathway toward independence, dignity and community development.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that women and many young people continue to face barriers including limited access to finance, business networks, markets, technology, training and entrepreneurial support.

Through this Programme, TJF commits to creating inclusive entrepreneurship ecosystems that enable women and underserved youth to establish, grow and sustain successful enterprises.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Promote inclusive entrepreneurship.
- Support women-led businesses.
- Expand opportunities for underserved youth.
- Reduce economic participation barriers.
- Strengthen community enterprise development.
- Encourage equal access to resources.

---

# ARTICLE II

# INCLUSIVE ENTREPRENEURSHIP VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To create an inclusive economic environment where women and young entrepreneurs have equal opportunities to innovate, build enterprises and contribute to sustainable economic growth."

# ARTICLE III

TJF shall:
