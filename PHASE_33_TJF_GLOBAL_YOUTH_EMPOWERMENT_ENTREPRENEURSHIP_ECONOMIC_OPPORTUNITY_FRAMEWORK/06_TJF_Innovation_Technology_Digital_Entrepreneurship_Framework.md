# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# INNOVATION, TECHNOLOGY AND DIGITAL ENTREPRENEURSHIP FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

Technology has transformed how individuals, businesses and communities create value.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that future economic opportunities will increasingly depend on digital skills, innovation capability, technology adoption and the ability to develop solutions using emerging technologies.

Through this Framework, TJF commits to building digital entrepreneurship ecosystems that enable young people to become technology innovators, digital business owners and contributors to the global digital economy.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Framework shall:

- Promote technology entrepreneurship.
- Encourage digital innovation.
- Develop digital business skills.
- Support technology startups.
- Expand participation in the digital economy.
- Encourage responsible technology development.

---

# ARTICLE II

# DIGITAL ENTREPRENEURSHIP VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To empower a generation of digital entrepreneurs who use technology, creativity and innovation to create businesses, solve problems and transform communities."

---

# ARTICLE III

# STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES

TJF shall:

- Increase access to digital entrepreneurship knowledge.
- Support technology-based enterprises.
- Develop innovation ecosystems.
- Promote emerging technology skills.
- Connect innovators with opportunities.
- Encourage responsible digital transformation.
