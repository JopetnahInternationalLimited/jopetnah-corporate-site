# Donor and Funding Partnership Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable development requires reliable, diversified and responsibly managed financial resources.

Strong donor relationships enable organizations to implement impactful programmes, expand community reach, invest in innovation and respond effectively to emerging development challenges.

This Donor and Funding Partnership Strategy establishes TJF’s approach for identifying funding opportunities, developing donor relationships, mobilizing resources and ensuring responsible stewardship of financial support.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s donor engagement framework.
- Strengthen resource mobilization capacity.
- Diversify funding sources.
- Improve donor relationship management.
- Promote transparent financial stewardship.
- Support sustainable programme financing.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to funding relationships with:

- Institutional donors.
- Development agencies.
- Foundations.
- Government funding programmes.
- International organizations.
- Corporate donors.
- Philanthropic partners.
- Social impact investors.

---

# 3. Funding Partnership Vision

To establish TJF as a trusted and accountable development partner capable of attracting sustainable resources for transformative programmes.

---

# 4. Funding Partnership Mission

To build long-term funding relationships based on transparency, impact, accountability and shared commitment to sustainable development.

---

# 5. Funding Principles

TJF donor partnerships shall follow:

## Transparency

Providing accurate information on funding use and results.

---

## Accountability

Maintaining strong financial and programme management systems.

---

## Alignment

Ensuring funded activities support TJF’s mission and strategic priorities.

---

## Integrity

Maintaining ethical fundraising practices.

---

## Impact

Prioritizing measurable development outcomes.

---

## Sustainability

Building funding models that support long-term programmes.
