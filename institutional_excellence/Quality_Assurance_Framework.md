# Quality Assurance Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes quality as a fundamental requirement for achieving sustainable social impact, maintaining stakeholder confidence and becoming a globally respected institution.

Quality assurance provides the systems, standards and review processes necessary to ensure that TJF programmes, operations and services consistently meet expected levels of excellence.

This Quality Assurance Framework establishes TJF’s approach to planning, monitoring, evaluating and improving quality across all institutional activities.

The Framework promotes a culture where quality is not viewed as a separate function, but as a shared responsibility across the entire organization.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF quality standards.
- Ensure consistent programme excellence.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Improve institutional effectiveness.
- Promote continuous learning.
- Protect stakeholder confidence.
