# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# BOARD OF TRUSTEES CHARTER AND RESPONSIBILITIES

---

# PREAMBLE

The Board of Trustees of The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) serves as the guardian of the Foundation’s mission, constitutional principles and institutional legacy.

Trustees are entrusted with the responsibility of ensuring that TJF remains faithful to its purpose while continuously adapting to changing global realities.

The Board shall act as:

- Custodian of the mission.
- Protector of institutional assets.
- Guardian of ethical standards.
- Strategic steward of future generations.

---

# ARTICLE I

# ESTABLISHMENT OF THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES

## Section 1: Creation of the Board

The Board of Trustees is hereby established as the supreme governing body of TJF.

The Board derives its authority from:

- TJF Founding Constitution.
- Institutional Charter.
- Governance Framework.

---

## Section 2: Board Purpose

The purpose of the Board is to:

- Protect TJF’s mission.
- Provide strategic direction.
- Ensure institutional sustainability.
- Maintain public trust.
- Safeguard long-term legacy.

---

# ARTICLE II

# BOARD COMPOSITION

## Section 1: Composition Principles

The Board shall consist of individuals selected based on:

- Integrity.
- Competence.
- Experience.
- Commitment to TJF values.
- Ability to contribute strategically.

---

## Section 2: Board Diversity

The Board should reflect diverse expertise including:

- Governance.
- Finance.
- Law.
- Business.
- Education.
- Technology.
- Community development.
- International relations.

---

## Section 3: Board Size

The Board shall determine its appropriate size based on:

- Institutional growth.
- Governance requirements.
- Strategic needs.

The Board shall maintain sufficient members to ensure effective oversight while preserving decision-making efficiency.

---

# ARTICLE III

# TRUSTEE QUALIFICATIONS

A Trustee should demonstrate:

## Character

- Strong ethical reputation.
- Commitment to service.
- Integrity in leadership.

---

## Competence

Relevant experience in areas such as:

- Strategic leadership.
- Finance.
- Development.
- Innovation.
- Institutional management.

---

## Commitment

Willingness to:

- Support TJF’s mission.
- Participate actively.
- Accept fiduciary responsibilities.

---

# ARTICLE IV

# APPOINTMENT OF TRUSTEES

## Section 1: Appointment Authority

Trustees shall be appointed through a transparent process approved by the Board.

---

## Section 2: Appointment Considerations

The appointment process shall consider:

- Skills requirements.
- Institutional needs.
- Ethical suitability.
- Long-term contribution.

---

## Section 3: Trustee Orientation

New Trustees shall receive:

- Governance orientation.
- Institutional history.
- Strategic documents.
- Fiduciary responsibility training.

---

# ARTICLE V

# TERM OF SERVICE

Trustee service shall be structured to ensure:

- Leadership continuity.
- Institutional stability.
- Periodic renewal.

The Board shall establish:

- Term limits.
- Renewal procedures.
- Transition processes.

---

# ARTICLE VI

# CORE DUTIES OF TRUSTEES

Trustees shall have the following responsibilities:

---

# 1. Mission Stewardship

Trustees shall:

- Protect TJF’s founding purpose.
- Prevent mission deviation.
- Ensure alignment with institutional values.

---

# 2. Strategic Oversight

Trustees shall:

- Approve long-term strategy.
- Review institutional progress.
- Guide future direction.

---

# 3. Financial Stewardship

Trustees shall:

- Protect institutional assets.
- Approve financial policies.
- Monitor sustainability.

---

# 4. Governance Oversight

Trustees shall:

- Maintain governance standards.
- Ensure accountability systems.
- Review leadership performance.

---

# 5. Risk Protection

Trustees shall:

- Identify institutional risks.
- Ensure mitigation systems.
- Protect reputation and continuity.

---

# ARTICLE VII

# TRUSTEE FIDUCIARY RESPONSIBILITIES

Trustees shall uphold:

## Duty of Care

Making informed and responsible decisions.

---

## Duty of Loyalty

Acting in TJF’s best interests.

---

## Duty of Responsibility

Protecting institutional resources and reputation.

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# BOARD POWERS

The Board shall have authority to:

- Approve institutional strategy.
- Appoint senior leadership.
- Approve major policies.
- Protect TJF assets.
- Establish governance committees.
- Approve significant partnerships.
- Review organisational performance.

---

# ARTICLE IX

# LIMITATIONS OF TRUSTEE AUTHORITY

Trustees shall not:

- Use TJF resources for personal benefit.
- Interfere improperly with operations.
- Violate institutional values.
- Compromise confidentiality.
- Abuse governance authority.

---

# ARTICLE X

# BOARD MEETINGS

## Section 1: Meeting Frequency

The Board shall meet regularly as required for effective governance.

---

## Section 2: Meeting Responsibilities

Board meetings shall address:

- Strategy.
- Finance.
- Risk.
- Performance.
- Institutional development.

---

## Section 3: Meeting Records

The Board shall maintain:

- Agendas.
- Minutes.
- Decisions.
- Action records.

---

# ARTICLE XI

# BOARD COMMITTEES

The Board may establish committees including:

- Governance Committee.
- Finance and Investment Committee.
- Audit and Risk Committee.
- Programme and Impact Committee.
- Leadership and Succession Committee.

Committees shall operate under approved mandates.

---

# ARTICLE XII

# TRUSTEE ETHICS

Trustees shall demonstrate:

- Honesty.
- Independence.
- Respect.
- Confidentiality.
- Accountability.

---

# ARTICLE XIII

# TRUSTEE REMOVAL OR RESIGNATION

A Trustee may leave service through:

- Voluntary resignation.
- Completion of term.
- Inability to perform duties.
- Ethical violations.
- Governance concerns.

Removal procedures shall follow due process.

---

# ARTICLE XIV

# BOARD PERFORMANCE REVIEW

The Board shall periodically evaluate:

- Governance effectiveness.
- Strategic contribution.
- Individual trustee performance.
- Institutional impact.

---

# ARTICLE XV

# LEGACY RESPONSIBILITY

The Board shall protect TJF for future generations by ensuring:

- Mission continuity.
- Financial sustainability.
- Knowledge preservation.
- Leadership succession.

---

# FINAL TRUSTEE DECLARATION

> "A Trustee of The Jopetnah Foundation does not own the institution; they serve as a guardian entrusted with protecting its purpose, values and future."
