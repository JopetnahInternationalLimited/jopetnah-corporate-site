# Sustainable Financing Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that achieving lasting social impact requires sustainable financial systems that extend beyond short-term funding cycles.

Traditional dependence on donations and grants may limit long-term programme expansion. Therefore, TJF is committed to developing diversified financing models that combine philanthropy, partnerships, investments, social enterprise activities and responsible financial management.

This Sustainable Financing Strategy establishes TJF’s approach for building a resilient financial ecosystem capable of supporting current programmes, future initiatives and long-term institutional growth.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s long-term financing approach.
- Reduce dependency on single funding sources.
- Develop diversified revenue streams.
- Strengthen financial resilience.
- Support sustainable programme expansion.
- Enable intergenerational impact.

---

This Strategy applies to:

- Grant financing.
- Donations.
- Corporate partnerships.
- Social enterprises.

- Endowment funds.
- Income-generating activities.
- Strategic financial partnerships.
