# Global Workforce and Talent Mobility Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that people are the foundation of institutional success.

As TJF expands internationally, it requires a workforce system capable of developing leaders, mobilizing expertise across borders and strengthening organizational capacity in every location.

This Global Workforce and Talent Mobility Framework establishes the principles, systems and strategies required to build a high-performing multinational workforce.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s global workforce strategy.
- Develop international leadership capacity.
- Support talent movement across locations.
- Strengthen employee development.
- Create a future-ready workforce.

---

# 2. Workforce Vision

To build a globally diverse, highly skilled and values-driven workforce capable of advancing TJF’s mission worldwide.

---

# 3. Workforce Mission

To attract, develop and empower talented individuals who contribute to sustainable transformation across communities and countries.

---

# 4. Workforce Philosophy

TJF believes:

> "Institutions grow globally when their people are developed locally and empowered internationally."

A strong global workforce requires:

- Competence.
- Diversity.
- Leadership.
- Collaboration.
- Continuous learning.
