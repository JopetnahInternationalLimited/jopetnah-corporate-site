Verify a Staff ID

Scan the QR code on a Jopetnah International Limited staff ID card, or enter the verification code printed on its back, to confirm the card is genuine and current.

The code is printed in small type on the back of the card, below the contact details.

What this page shows

Only the holder’s name, job title, department, staff number and whether the card is currently valid. No contact details, home address or ID numbers are published.
