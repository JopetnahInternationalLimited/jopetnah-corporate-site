# Impact Investment Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes impact investment as an innovative approach that combines financial sustainability with measurable social and environmental outcomes.

Impact investment enables organizations to direct capital toward initiatives that generate positive change while maintaining responsible financial performance.

This Impact Investment Strategy establishes TJF’s framework for attracting, evaluating, managing and scaling investments that advance sustainable development, economic empowerment and community transformation.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s impact investment approach.
- Mobilize mission-aligned investment capital.
- Support sustainable enterprises.
- Create measurable social outcomes.
- Strengthen financial independence.
- Promote responsible investment practices.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to:

- Impact investment funds.
- Social enterprises.
- Sustainable businesses.
- Development finance partnerships.
- Blended finance initiatives.
- Mission-aligned investments.

---

# 3. Impact Investment Vision

To become a trusted impact investment institution that channels capital toward solutions improving lives, strengthening communities and protecting the planet.

---

# 4. Impact Investment Mission

To combine financial discipline with social purpose by investing in sustainable initiatives that create measurable and lasting impact.

---

# 5. Impact Investment Principles

TJF investments shall follow:

## Impact Before Profit Maximization

Financial returns must support, not replace, social objectives.

---

## Responsible Capital Allocation

Investments must create meaningful development outcomes.

---

## Risk Awareness

Investment decisions must include proper assessment.

---

## Transparency

Investment activities must be clearly reported.

---

## Sustainability

Investments should generate long-term value.

---

## Inclusion

Priority should be given to underserved communities.

---

# 6. Strategic Objectives

TJF seeks to:

- Build an impact investment portfolio.
- Support mission-aligned enterprises.
- Attract responsible investors.
- Encourage sustainable innovation.
- Create employment opportunities.
- Generate long-term financial returns.

# 10. Investment Evaluation Criteria

Investment opportunities shall be assessed based on:

## Impact Potential

Questions:

- What social problem is addressed?
- How many people may benefit?
- What measurable outcomes are expected?

---

## Financial Sustainability

Assessment includes:

- Revenue model.
- Growth potential.
- Financial projections.

---

## Management Capacity

Assessment includes:

- Leadership capability.
- Operational strength.
- Governance systems.

---

## Strategic Alignment

The investment must support TJF priorities.

---
