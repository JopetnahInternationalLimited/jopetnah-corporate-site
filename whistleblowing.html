Governance & Compliance

Whistleblowing / Speak-Up Policy

We encourage anyone with a genuine concern about misconduct to speak up. Reports are handled sensitively and without retaliation for good-faith reporting.

Purpose

A Safe Route to Speak Up

Jopetnah International Limited encourages employees and stakeholders to raise genuine concerns about misconduct, unlawful activity, corruption, fraud, safety risks or serious breaches of Company standards. Speaking up helps protect people, the Company, clients, partners and communities.

This Policy applies to employees, directors, contractors, consultants, suppliers, clients, partners and other stakeholders who have a genuine concern connected with Company activities.

Scope

What Should Be Reported

  • Bribery, corruption, kickbacks, fraud or financial misconduct.
  • Serious breaches of the Code of Ethics, Anti-Corruption Policy or other Company requirements.
  • Criminal conduct or serious breaches of applicable law.
  • Health, safety or environmental risks that could cause serious harm.
  • Conflicts of interest, procurement manipulation or undisclosed improper relationships.
  • Misuse of Company funds, assets, confidential information or personal data.
  • Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, forced labour, child labour or other serious workplace misconduct.
  • Attempts to conceal, destroy evidence of or retaliate against concerns about any of the above.
Reporting Channels

How to Raise a Concern

Supervisor or Manager

Raise the concern with your supervisor or departmental head where it is appropriate and safe to do so.

Senior Leadership

Concerns may be directed to the Managing Director, Director of Human Resources & Public Relations or Company Secretary.

Confidential Email

Email jopetnah.intl.ltd@gmail.com and, where appropriate, use the subject line “Confidential – Speak-Up Concern”.

Telephone / Contact Page

Use an official Company telephone number or the Contact page to request a confidential conversation.

Reports may be made anonymously where permitted and practical. Providing contact information can help investigators clarify facts and provide appropriate feedback.

Protection

Confidentiality & Non-Retaliation

Reports will be handled sensitively and confidentially to the fullest extent practicable, while recognising that disclosure may be necessary to investigate, protect people, comply with law or take corrective action.

No person who raises a genuine concern in good faith should suffer retaliation, victimisation, discrimination or adverse employment consequences. Retaliation is itself a serious breach of Company standards.

A person who knowingly makes a deliberately false or malicious allegation may face appropriate action. A concern should not be dismissed merely because it cannot ultimately be substantiated.

Process

What Happens After a Report

  • The report is received and, where contact details are available, acknowledgement may be provided.
  • An initial assessment determines whether the matter falls within this Policy and what response is appropriate.
  • A suitable person or team conducts an impartial review or investigation, taking account of conflicts of interest.
  • Relevant records and evidence are protected and handled appropriately.
  • The reporter may be asked for clarification or supporting information.
  • Corrective, disciplinary, contractual or other action may be taken where warranted.
  • Feedback may be provided to the reporter where appropriate, lawful and practicable.
  • Records are retained securely in accordance with applicable requirements.
External Reporting

When Other Channels May Be Appropriate

Nothing in this Policy is intended to prevent a person from making a report to a competent regulator, law-enforcement body, court or other lawful authority where applicable. Where there is an immediate threat to life, safety or security, appropriate emergency or competent-authority channels should be used.

Governance

Responsibility & Review

Overall responsibility rests with the Managing Director, supported by senior management and the Company’s governance structures. The Company will review this Policy periodically and update it as its operations, risks and legal obligations evolve.
