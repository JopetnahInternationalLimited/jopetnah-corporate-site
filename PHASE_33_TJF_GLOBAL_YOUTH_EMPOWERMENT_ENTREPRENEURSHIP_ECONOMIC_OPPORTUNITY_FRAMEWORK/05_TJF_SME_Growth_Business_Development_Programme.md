# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# SME GROWTH AND BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

Small and medium enterprises represent a major foundation of economic activity, employment creation and innovation worldwide.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that many entrepreneurs and small businesses face challenges including limited business knowledge, weak operational systems, restricted access to finance, inadequate technology adoption and limited market opportunities.

Through this Programme, TJF commits to strengthening SME ecosystems by providing business development support, capacity building, market connections and pathways for sustainable enterprise growth.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Support SME development.
- Improve business management capacity.
- Strengthen enterprise sustainability.
- Promote innovation and technology adoption.
- Expand market opportunities.
- Encourage job creation through business growth.
