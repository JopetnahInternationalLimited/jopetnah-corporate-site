# Renewable Energy Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes access to clean, affordable and reliable energy as a critical driver of sustainable development, economic growth, improved livelihoods and environmental protection.

Energy challenges, including limited access, dependence on fossil fuels and rising climate risks, continue to affect communities worldwide. TJF therefore promotes renewable energy solutions that increase energy access, reduce environmental impacts and strengthen community resilience.

This Renewable Energy Strategy establishes TJF’s commitment to supporting renewable energy adoption, clean technology innovation, energy efficiency and sustainable energy systems across its operations, programmes and partnerships.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s renewable energy priorities.
- Promote clean energy solutions.
- Reduce dependence on environmentally harmful energy sources.
- Improve energy access for communities.
- Support climate mitigation efforts.
- Encourage renewable energy innovation and investment.
