# Sustainable Agriculture Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes agriculture as a critical foundation for food security, livelihoods, economic development and community resilience.

However, agriculture faces increasing challenges including climate change, land degradation, declining soil fertility, water scarcity and changing market conditions. TJF therefore promotes sustainable agricultural approaches that improve productivity while protecting natural resources and strengthening farmer livelihoods.

This Sustainable Agriculture Framework establishes TJF’s commitment to supporting climate-smart agriculture, environmentally responsible farming, agricultural innovation, value-chain development and resilient food systems.

---

This Framework aims to:

- Promote sustainable agricultural practices.

TJF shall promote practices that:

- Reduce climate risks.
- Lower environmental impacts.

Examples include:

- Sustainable soil management.
