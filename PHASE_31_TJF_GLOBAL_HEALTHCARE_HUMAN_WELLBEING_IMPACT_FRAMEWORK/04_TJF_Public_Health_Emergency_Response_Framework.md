# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# PUBLIC HEALTH EMERGENCY RESPONSE FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

Public health emergencies can significantly affect lives, communities, economies and healthcare systems.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes the importance of preparedness, rapid response, coordination and resilience in protecting communities during health crises.

This Framework establishes TJF's emergency health response approach to support communities before, during and after public health emergencies.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Framework shall:

- Establish TJF emergency health response systems.
- Strengthen preparedness capabilities.
- Support rapid healthcare interventions.
- Coordinate emergency partnerships.
- Protect vulnerable communities.
- Improve long-term health resilience.

---

# ARTICLE II

# EMERGENCY RESPONSE VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To build a globally prepared and responsive healthcare system capable of protecting communities and saving lives during public health emergencies."

---

# ARTICLE III

# GUIDING PRINCIPLES

TJF emergency health response shall follow:

## 1. Preparedness

Planning before emergencies occur.

---

## 2. Speed

Providing timely and effective responses.

---

## 3. Coordination

Working with relevant stakeholders.

---

## 4. Humanitarian Responsibility

Prioritizing protection of human life and dignity.

---

## 5. Evidence-Based Response

Using reliable information and scientific guidance.

---

## 6. Community Protection

Ensuring vulnerable populations receive support.
