# Environmental Sustainability Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes environmental sustainability as a fundamental requirement for achieving inclusive, resilient and long-term development.

Climate change, biodiversity loss, environmental degradation and unsustainable resource use continue to affect communities, economies and ecosystems worldwide. TJF therefore commits to integrating environmental responsibility across its governance, programmes, operations and partnerships.

This Environmental Sustainability Policy establishes TJF’s commitment to protecting natural resources, reducing environmental impact, promoting sustainable practices and supporting communities in building climate-resilient futures.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s environmental sustainability commitments.
- Integrate environmental responsibility into all operations.
- Reduce negative environmental impacts.
- Promote sustainable development practices.
- Support climate-resilient communities.
- Encourage environmental awareness and innovation.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- TJF headquarters.
- Regional and country offices.
- Projects and programmes.
- Events and activities.
- Supply chains.
- Partnerships.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.

---

# 3. Environmental Sustainability Vision

To become an environmentally responsible global foundation that promotes sustainable development, protects ecosystems and strengthens climate resilience.

---

# 4. Environmental Sustainability Mission

To integrate environmental stewardship into every aspect of TJF’s work while supporting communities and partners to create sustainable solutions for people and the planet.

# 5. Policy Principles

TJF environmental sustainability shall be guided by:

## Environmental Responsibility

TJF shall minimize harm to the environment and promote responsible resource use.

---

## Climate Action

TJF shall contribute to climate mitigation and adaptation efforts.

---

## Resource Efficiency

TJF shall promote efficient use of:

- Energy.
- Water.
- Materials.
- Natural resources.

---

## Pollution Prevention

TJF shall reduce waste, emissions and environmental risks.

---

## Ecosystem Protection

TJF shall support conservation of biodiversity and natural ecosystems.
