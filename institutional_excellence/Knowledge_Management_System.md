# Knowledge Management System

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes knowledge as one of its most valuable institutional assets.

Every programme implemented, partnership developed, challenge overcome and lesson learned contributes to TJF’s collective intelligence.

This Knowledge Management System establishes the structures, processes and technologies required to capture, organize, preserve, share and apply knowledge across TJF.

The System enables TJF to become a learning organization capable of continuously improving its operations, strengthening decision-making and increasing global impact.

---

# 1. Purpose

This System aims to:

- Preserve institutional knowledge.
- Strengthen organizational learning.
- Improve decision-making.
- Support innovation.
- Promote knowledge sharing.
- Build institutional intelligence.

---

# 2. Scope

This System applies to:

- Board members.
- Executive leadership.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Researchers.
- Programme teams.
- Regional offices.
- Strategic partners.

To establish TJF as a globally recognized learning institution where knowledge drives innovation, excellence and sustainable transformation.

---

# 4. Knowledge Management Mission

To create systems that capture, protect, share and apply knowledge to strengthen TJF’s institutional capabilities and global impact.
