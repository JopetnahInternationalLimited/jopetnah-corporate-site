# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ADOPTION STRATEGY

---

# PREAMBLE

Artificial Intelligence is transforming the way institutions operate, make decisions and deliver services.

The Jopetnah Foundation is committed to adopting Artificial Intelligence responsibly to improve humanitarian outcomes, operational excellence, innovation and global collaboration.

AI shall complement—not replace—human judgement, compassion and accountability.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Strategy establishes the institutional framework for:

- Responsible AI adoption.
- Ethical AI governance.
- AI risk management.
- Innovation.
- Capacity development.
- Sustainable digital transformation.

---

# ARTICLE II

# TJF AI VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To responsibly harness Artificial Intelligence to improve lives, strengthen institutions and accelerate sustainable development while preserving human dignity, fairness and accountability."

---

# ARTICLE III

# GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Artificial Intelligence within TJF shall always be:

### Human-Centred

Technology shall serve people.

---

### Ethical

AI shall respect human rights and dignity.

---

### Transparent

Important AI-supported decisions shall be explainable where reasonably practicable.

---

### Fair

AI shall not intentionally discriminate against individuals or communities.

---

### Secure

AI systems shall protect institutional and beneficiary information.

---

### Accountable

Humans remain responsible for decisions supported by AI.

---

# ARTICLE V

Artificial Intelligence may support:

## Programme Management

- Programme planning.

- Trend analysis.

# ARTICLE VI

# PROHIBITED AI USES

TJF shall prohibit AI use for:

- Unlawful surveillance.
- Deliberate misinformation.
- Manipulative or deceptive practices.
- Discriminatory decision-making.
- Unauthorized processing of confidential information.
- Activities contrary to international human rights principles.

---

# ARTICLE VII

# HUMAN OVERSIGHT

Human oversight shall be mandatory for:

- Financial approvals.
- Employment decisions.
- Procurement awards.
- Strategic planning.
- Legal matters.
- High-impact beneficiary decisions.

AI recommendations shall support—not replace—authorized decision-makers.

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# AI ETHICS FRAMEWORK

The Foundation shall ensure that AI systems are:

- Lawful.
- Ethical.
- Safe.
- Reliable.
- Inclusive.
- Environmentally responsible where feasible.

Potential harms shall be identified and mitigated before deployment.

---

# ARTICLE IX

# AI RISK MANAGEMENT

Potential risks include:

| Risk | Mitigation |
|-------|------------|
| Bias | Regular testing and review |
| Privacy breaches | Strong security controls |
| Inaccurate outputs | Human verification |
| Cybersecurity threats | Secure AI infrastructure |
| Overreliance on AI | Mandatory human oversight |
| Reputational harm | Ethical review process |

---

# ARTICLE X

# DATA REQUIREMENTS

AI systems shall use data that is:

- Lawfully obtained.
- Accurate.
- Relevant.
- Securely stored.
- Properly governed.

The AI Strategy shall operate alongside the TJF Data Governance Framework.

---

# ARTICLE XI

# AI PROCUREMENT

Before adopting AI technologies, TJF shall assess:

- Security.
- Vendor reputation.
- Compliance.
- Transparency.
- Cost-effectiveness.
- Technical support.
- Long-term sustainability.

---

# ARTICLE XII

# STAFF CAPACITY DEVELOPMENT

The Foundation shall develop AI literacy through:

- Introductory AI awareness.
- Responsible AI training.
- Prompt engineering.
- Data literacy.
- AI ethics.
- Cybersecurity awareness.

Leadership shall receive specialized AI governance training.

---

# ARTICLE XIII

# AI RESEARCH AND INNOVATION

TJF shall encourage responsible experimentation in areas such as:

- Climate resilience.
- Education.
- Agriculture.
- Public health.
- Financial inclusion.
- Humanitarian response.
- Social innovation.

Research shall follow approved ethical review processes.

---

# ARTICLE XIV

# MONITORING AND EVALUATION

AI initiatives shall be evaluated using indicators such as:

- Productivity improvements.
- Cost efficiency.
- User satisfaction.
- Accuracy.
- Risk incidents.
- Compliance.
- Social impact.

Regular reviews shall inform future improvements.

---

# ARTICLE XV

# PARTNERSHIPS

The Foundation may collaborate with:

- Universities.
- Research institutions.
- Technology companies.
- International organizations.
- Innovation hubs.
- Government agencies.

Collaborations shall align with TJF values and governance standards.

---

# ARTICLE XVI

# CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

The AI Strategy shall be reviewed to reflect:

- Technological advances.
- Regulatory developments.
- Ethical best practices.
- Institutional lessons learned.

---

# ARTICLE XVII

# AI ROADMAP (2026–2100)

### Phase I

Digital readiness and AI awareness.

### Phase II

Operational AI integration.

### Phase III

Predictive analytics and intelligent decision support.

### Phase IV

AI-enabled global programme optimization.

### Phase V

Trusted AI leadership in international development.

---

# ARTICLE XVIII

# FUTURE AI VISION

By 2100, TJF aspires to become an internationally recognized organization that responsibly applies Artificial Intelligence to expand opportunity, strengthen communities and improve lives while ensuring that every significant decision remains grounded in human values, accountability and compassion.

---

# FINAL DECLARATION

> "Artificial Intelligence is a powerful tool, but humanity remains our greatest strength. At The Jopetnah Foundation, technology will always serve people—not the other way around."
