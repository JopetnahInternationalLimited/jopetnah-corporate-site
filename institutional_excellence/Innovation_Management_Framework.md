# Innovation Management Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes innovation as a strategic capability essential for solving complex challenges, improving institutional effectiveness and creating sustainable transformation.

Innovation enables TJF to move beyond traditional approaches by encouraging creativity, experimentation, technology adoption and collaborative problem-solving.

This Innovation Management Framework establishes the systems, governance structures and processes required to identify, develop, test and scale innovative solutions across TJF operations and programmes.

The Framework positions TJF as a future-ready institution committed to continuous advancement and global impact.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish an institutional innovation culture.
- Encourage creative problem-solving.
- Support research and experimentation.
- Promote technology adoption.
- Develop scalable solutions.
- Strengthen TJF’s future readiness.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Board leadership.
- Executive management.
- Departments.
- Programme teams.
- Research units.
- Technology teams.
- Strategic partners.
- Innovation collaborators.

To position TJF as a globally recognized innovation-driven institution that develops sustainable solutions for social and economic transformation.
