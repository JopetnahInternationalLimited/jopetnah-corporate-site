# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# FOOD PROCESSING AND AGRICULTURAL INDUSTRIALIZATION STRATEGY

---

# PREAMBLE

Agricultural transformation requires more than increasing production. Sustainable food systems depend on the ability to process, preserve, package and distribute agricultural products efficiently.

Many agricultural communities lose significant economic opportunities because raw materials are exported or sold without value addition.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes agricultural industrialization as a pathway for:

- Job creation.
- Economic growth.
- Food security.
- Rural transformation.
- Enterprise development.

Through this Strategy, TJF commits to supporting food processing industries and agricultural manufacturing ecosystems that create lasting economic value.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Strategy shall:

- Promote agricultural value addition.
- Support food processing enterprises.
- Strengthen agricultural industries.
- Reduce post-harvest losses.
- Improve market competitiveness.
- Create employment opportunities.
