# UN Engagement Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes the United Nations (UN) as a central global platform for advancing peace, sustainable development, humanitarian cooperation and international collaboration.

The United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) provide a shared global framework addressing critical challenges including poverty, inequality, health, education, climate change and economic development.

This UN Engagement Strategy establishes TJF’s approach for aligning its programmes, research, partnerships and advocacy efforts with UN priorities while creating pathways for meaningful participation in global development initiatives.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s UN engagement framework.
- Align TJF programmes with global development priorities.
- Strengthen collaboration with UN agencies.
- Support SDG implementation.
- Increase international visibility.
- Create opportunities for global cooperation.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to engagement with:

- United Nations agencies.
- UN programmes and initiatives.
- UN development platforms.
- SDG networks.
- International conferences.
- Global policy forums.

---

# 3. UN Engagement Vision

To become a recognized development partner contributing knowledge, innovation and solutions toward achieving global sustainable development goals.
