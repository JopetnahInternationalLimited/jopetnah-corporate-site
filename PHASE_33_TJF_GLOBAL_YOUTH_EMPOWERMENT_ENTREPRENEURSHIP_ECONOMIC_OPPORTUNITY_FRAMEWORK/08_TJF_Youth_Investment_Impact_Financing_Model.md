# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# YOUTH INVESTMENT AND IMPACT FINANCING MODEL

---

# PREAMBLE

Access to appropriate financing remains one of the greatest challenges facing emerging entrepreneurs and youth-led enterprises.

Many promising business ideas fail to reach their potential due to limited access to startup capital, investment networks, financial guidance and growth resources.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes the importance of creating responsible financing systems that support entrepreneurship while generating measurable social and economic impact.

Through this Model, TJF commits to developing youth-focused investment pathways that transform innovative ideas into sustainable enterprises.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Model shall:

- Establish TJF youth investment mechanisms.
- Support youth-led enterprise growth.
- Promote impact-driven financing.
- Connect entrepreneurs with capital opportunities.
- Encourage responsible investment practices.
- Build sustainable economic ecosystems.
