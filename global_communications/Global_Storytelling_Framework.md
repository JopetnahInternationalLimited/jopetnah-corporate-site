# Global Storytelling Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes storytelling as one of the most powerful tools for connecting people, communicating impact and inspiring collective action.

Beyond statistics and programme reports, stories reveal the human experiences behind development challenges and demonstrate how sustainable solutions create meaningful change.

This Global Storytelling Framework establishes TJF’s approach for creating authentic, ethical and impactful stories that communicate community voices, programme outcomes and the Foundation’s global mission.

The Framework ensures that every story shared by TJF reflects dignity, accuracy, hope and measurable transformation.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s storytelling standards.
- Strengthen impact communication.
- Highlight community experiences.
- Support fundraising and partnerships.
- Increase public understanding of TJF programmes.
- Build emotional connections with global audiences.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Written stories.
- Photography.
- Videos.
- Documentaries.
- Social media content.
- Reports.
- Campaign materials.
- Public presentations.

---

# 3. Storytelling Vision

To become a globally respected storyteller of human transformation, community resilience and sustainable development impact.

---

# 4. Storytelling Mission

To share authentic stories that inspire understanding, encourage action and demonstrate the positive change created through TJF initiatives.

---

# 5. Storytelling Principles

TJF storytelling shall follow:

## Human Dignity

People shall always be represented respectfully and positively.

---

## Authenticity

Stories must reflect real experiences.

---

## Consent

Individuals must provide appropriate permission before being featured.

---

## Accuracy

Information must be truthful and verified.

---

## Empowerment

Stories should highlight strength, resilience and opportunity.

---

## Impact Focus

Stories should demonstrate meaningful outcomes.

---

TJF seeks to:

# 17. Ethical Storytelling Standards

TJF shall avoid:

- Exploitation of vulnerable individuals.
- Misrepresentation.
- Sensationalism.
- Unverified claims.
- Harmful imagery.

TJF shall promote:

- Respect.
- Accuracy.
- Empowerment.
- Transparency.

# 18. Storytelling Risk Management

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Privacy concerns | Consent procedures |
| Misrepresentation | Verification process |
| Negative stereotypes | Dignity-based storytelling |
| Inaccurate information | Editorial review |
| Audience misunderstanding | Clear context |
