# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# CARBON MANAGEMENT STRATEGY

---

# PREAMBLE

Climate change requires institutions to understand, manage and reduce their contribution to greenhouse gas emissions.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that responsible climate leadership requires measurable action, transparent reporting and continuous improvement.

This Strategy establishes TJF's approach to carbon measurement, emissions reduction, climate responsibility and long-term carbon neutrality.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Strategy shall:

- Establish TJF's carbon management framework.
- Measure institutional emissions.
- Reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
- Promote low-carbon operations.
- Support climate accountability.
- Guide long-term carbon neutrality efforts.

---

# ARTICLE II

# CARBON MANAGEMENT VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To become a climate-responsible institution that continuously reduces its carbon footprint while supporting sustainable development and environmental restoration."

---

# ARTICLE III

# CARBON MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

TJF carbon management shall follow:

## 1. Measurement Before Management

Emissions must be understood before effective reduction strategies are implemented.

---

## 2. Reduction First

Priority shall be given to reducing emissions at source.

---

## 3. Transparency

Carbon-related information shall be accurately reported.

---

## 4. Continuous Improvement

Carbon performance shall improve over time.

---

## 5. Responsibility

All departments contribute to carbon reduction.

---

# ARTICLE V

# GREENHOUSE GAS ACCOUNTING FRAMEWORK

TJF shall establish systems to measure emissions according to recognized greenhouse gas accounting principles.

Carbon accounting shall consider:

- Direct emissions.
- Energy-related indirect emissions.
- Relevant value chain emissions.

---

# ARTICLE VI

# EMISSION CATEGORIES

## Scope 1 — Direct Emissions

Including emissions from sources controlled by TJF, such as:

- Owned vehicles.
- Institutional equipment.
- Direct fuel consumption.

---

## Scope 2 — Energy-Related Emissions

Including emissions associated with:

- Purchased electricity.
- Energy consumption in facilities.

---

## Scope 3 — Indirect Value Chain Emissions

Including relevant activities such as:

- Business travel.
- Procurement.
- Supply chains.
- Waste management.
- Employee commuting where applicable.

---

# ARTICLE VII

# CARBON MEASUREMENT SYSTEM

TJF shall establish mechanisms for tracking:

- Energy consumption.
- Fuel usage.
- Transportation impacts.
- Facility emissions.
- Waste-related emissions.
- Programme-related emissions where appropriate.

Data shall support informed decision-making and sustainability reporting.

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# CARBON REDUCTION STRATEGY

TJF shall reduce emissions through:

## Energy Efficiency

- Efficient equipment.
- Responsible energy use.
- Smart energy management.

---

## Renewable Energy

- Solar energy adoption.
- Clean energy partnerships.
- Renewable technology solutions.

---

## Sustainable Transport

- Reduced unnecessary travel.
- Efficient transport planning.
- Lower-emission mobility options.

---

## Digital Transformation

- Remote collaboration.
- Digital documentation.
- Reduced physical resource consumption.

---

## Sustainable Facilities

- Green buildings.
- Efficient infrastructure.
- Responsible maintenance.

---

# ARTICLE IX

# CARBON REDUCTION TARGETS

TJF shall establish progressive targets including:

### Short-Term (2026–2035)

- Establish carbon baseline.
- Implement measurement systems.
- Reduce operational emissions.

---

### Medium-Term (2036–2050)

- Expand renewable energy use.
- Improve low-carbon operations.
- Strengthen carbon reporting.

---

### Long-Term (2051–2100)

- Achieve highly optimized low-carbon operations.
- Support carbon-positive initiatives.
- Contribute to environmental restoration.

---

# ARTICLE X

# CARBON OFFSETTING PRINCIPLES

Where emissions cannot reasonably be eliminated, TJF may consider responsible carbon offset approaches including:

- Reforestation initiatives.
- Ecosystem restoration.
- Verified climate projects.
- Community environmental programmes.

Offsetting shall complement, not replace, emission reduction efforts.

---

# ARTICLE XI

# LOW-CARBON PROGRAMMES

TJF programmes may support:

- Climate-smart agriculture.
- Renewable energy access.
- Environmental restoration.
- Sustainable livelihoods.
- Green entrepreneurship.

---

# ARTICLE XII

# CARBON DATA MANAGEMENT

Carbon data shall be managed through:

- Digital monitoring systems.
- Data verification procedures.
- Performance dashboards.
- Periodic reviews.

Carbon data shall align with TJF's Digital Impact Measurement Platform.

---

# ARTICLE XIII

# REPORTING AND DISCLOSURE

TJF shall communicate:

- Carbon performance.
- Reduction initiatives.
- Sustainability achievements.
- Climate challenges.
- Future commitments.

Reporting shall promote transparency and accountability.

---

# ARTICLE XIV

# STAFF AND COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

Carbon responsibility shall be strengthened through:

- Employee awareness.
- Sustainability training.
- Community education.
- Environmental campaigns.

---

# ARTICLE XV

# CARBON RISK MANAGEMENT

Carbon-related risks shall include:

- Regulatory changes.
- Energy cost fluctuations.
- Climate impacts.
- Supply chain emissions.
- Operational vulnerabilities.

Appropriate mitigation measures shall be developed.

---

# ARTICLE XVI

# PARTNERSHIPS

TJF may collaborate with:

- Climate organizations.
- Research institutions.
- Governments.
- Environmental specialists.
- Sustainable finance partners.

---

# ARTICLE XVII

# CARBON MANAGEMENT ROADMAP TO 2100

## 2026–2035: Foundation Stage

- Establish carbon governance.
- Create emissions baseline.
- Begin reduction initiatives.

---

## 2036–2050: Acceleration Stage

- Scale renewable energy.
- Expand carbon reduction programmes.
- Strengthen climate partnerships.

---

## 2051–2100: Climate Leadership Stage

- Operate as a globally recognized low-carbon institution.
- Support regenerative environmental solutions.
- Promote climate innovation worldwide.

---

# ARTICLE XVIII

# FINAL DECLARATION

> "Managing carbon is managing our responsibility to future generations. Through measurement, innovation and purposeful action, The Jopetnah Foundation commits to reducing its environmental footprint and supporting a sustainable planet."
