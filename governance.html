Accountability in Practice

Governance Is Something We Practise, Not Just Publish

From documented decisions to signed agreements, our governance commitments are reflected in how our team works day to day — not only in the policies on this page.

Principles

Our Governance Approach

We are committed to operating with integrity, transparency and accountability. Our governance framework is designed to be practical and appropriate to our current size while providing a solid foundation for responsible growth.

  • Clear lines of responsibility and decision-making
  • Ethical conduct in all business dealings
  • Compliance with applicable Ugandan laws and regulations
  • Progressive development of formal policies and controls
  • Accountability to clients, partners, employees and other stakeholders
Oversight

Board of Directors

The Board (or equivalent oversight body) provides strategic direction, oversight and accountability. It is responsible for ensuring the organisation operates with integrity, manages key risks, and pursues sustainable growth.

Structure

Organisational Framework

Our structure is intentionally straightforward to maintain clear accountability. It brings together central corporate oversight and four complementary operating divisions to coordinate capabilities and deliver practical solutions.

SHAREHOLDERSOwnership and reserved shareholder decisions
BOARD OF DIRECTORSStrategic direction, governance and oversight
MANAGING DIRECTOR (MD)Executive leadership and overall managementSupporting leadership roles: Director – Human Resources & Public Relations; Company Secretary (profiles to be published)

Independent Audit, Risk & Compliance Assurance

Internal Audit maintains functional accountability to the Board or its Audit Committee. Administrative coordination must not compromise its independence.
