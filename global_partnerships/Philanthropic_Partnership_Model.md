# Philanthropic Partnership Model

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes philanthropy as a powerful force for creating sustainable social transformation.

Individuals, family foundations, charitable organizations and impact-focused philanthropists play an important role in supporting innovative solutions, empowering communities and accelerating progress toward sustainable development.

This Philanthropic Partnership Model establishes TJF’s approach for building meaningful relationships with philanthropic partners while ensuring transparency, accountability and measurable impact.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Model aims to:

- Establish TJF’s philanthropic engagement framework.
- Build long-term relationships with philanthropists.
- Mobilize transformational support.
- Create meaningful giving opportunities.
- Strengthen donor confidence.
- Maximize social impact.

---

# 2. Scope

This Model applies to relationships with:

- Individual philanthropists.
- High-net-worth individuals.
- Family foundations.
- Giving circles.
- Charitable trusts.
- Impact-focused donors.
- Philanthropic networks.

---

# 3. Philanthropic Vision

To create a global community of compassionate partners committed to advancing sustainable development and improving lives.

---

# 4. Philanthropic Mission

To connect philanthropic resources with impactful programmes that create measurable and lasting positive change.

# 5. Philanthropic Partnership Principles

TJF philanthropic partnerships shall follow:

## Impact

---

## Stewardship

## Legacy

Creating long-term value for future generations.
