# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# SUSTAINABLE PROCUREMENT FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that procurement decisions have environmental, social and economic consequences.

Responsible procurement ensures that goods, services and works acquired by the Foundation contribute to sustainable development while maintaining quality, transparency, value for money and ethical standards.

This Framework integrates sustainability considerations into TJF procurement processes across all countries and operations.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Framework shall:

- Establish sustainable procurement principles.
- Promote responsible supply chains.
- Reduce environmental impacts.
- Encourage ethical suppliers.
- Support local and inclusive economic participation.
- Strengthen procurement transparency.

---

# ARTICLE II

# SUSTAINABLE PROCUREMENT VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To build a responsible global supply chain where every procurement decision creates value for communities, protects the environment and advances sustainable development."

---

# ARTICLE III

# GUIDING PRINCIPLES

TJF sustainable procurement shall be guided by:

## 1. Value for Money

Achieving the best balance of quality, cost, sustainability and long-term benefit.

---

## 2. Environmental Responsibility

Reducing environmental impacts throughout the supply chain.

---

## 3. Ethical Conduct

Maintaining integrity, fairness and transparency.

---

## 4. Social Responsibility

Supporting human dignity, inclusion and responsible labour practices.

---

## 5. Lifecycle Thinking

Considering impacts from acquisition through disposal.

---

## 6. Continuous Improvement

Improving procurement sustainability performance over time.

---

---

# ARTICLE V

# SUSTAINABLE PROCUREMENT CRITERIA

Procurement decisions shall consider:

- Product quality.
- Total lifecycle cost.
- Environmental impact.
- Supplier sustainability practices.
- Social responsibility.
- Innovation potential.
- Local economic benefits.

---

# ARTICLE VI

# GREEN PROCUREMENT STANDARDS

Where practical, TJF shall prioritize products and services that:

- Use sustainable materials.
- Minimize waste.
- Reduce energy consumption.
- Have longer useful lifespans.
- Support recycling or reuse.
- Reduce environmental harm.

---

# ARTICLE VII

# SUPPLIER SUSTAINABILITY REQUIREMENTS

Suppliers may be assessed on:

## Environmental Practices

Including:

- Waste management.
- Energy efficiency.
- Pollution prevention.
- Environmental compliance.

---

## Social Standards

Including:

- Fair labour practices.
- Workplace safety.
- Non-discrimination.
- Respect for human rights.

---

## Governance Standards

Including:

- Ethical conduct.
- Anti-corruption practices.
- Transparency.
- Regulatory compliance.

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# RESPONSIBLE SOURCING

TJF shall encourage responsible sourcing through:

- Supplier due diligence.
- Ethical supply chain assessments.
- Traceability where appropriate.
- Preference for responsible producers.

---

# ARTICLE IX

# LOCAL AND INCLUSIVE PROCUREMENT

Where feasible, TJF shall promote:

- Local supplier participation.
- Small and medium enterprise engagement.
- Youth and women-led enterprise opportunities.
- Community economic development.

Local procurement shall remain consistent with quality, transparency and value-for-money requirements.

---

# ARTICLE X

# SUSTAINABLE CONTRACTING

Procurement contracts may include:

- Environmental requirements.
- Sustainability commitments.
- Reporting obligations.
- Ethical compliance clauses.
- Waste management requirements.

---

# ARTICLE XI

# SUPPLIER EVALUATION

Supplier assessment may consider:

- Sustainability performance.
- Compliance history.
- Environmental practices.
- Social responsibility.
- Delivery performance.
- Innovation capability.

---

# ARTICLE XII

# SUPPLY CHAIN RISK MANAGEMENT

TJF shall identify and manage risks including:

- Environmental harm.
- Labour violations.
- Supplier dependency.
- Supply disruptions.
- Ethical concerns.
- Regulatory changes.

---

# ARTICLE XIII

# DIGITAL PROCUREMENT

The Foundation shall promote:

- Electronic procurement systems.
- Digital supplier records.
- Transparent approval workflows.
- Data-driven supplier monitoring.

Digital procurement shall support efficiency and accountability.

---

# ARTICLE XIV

# SUSTAINABLE PROCUREMENT TRAINING

Procurement personnel shall receive training on:

- Green procurement principles.
- Supplier evaluation.
- Ethical sourcing.
- Sustainability requirements.
- Contract management.

---

# ARTICLE XV

# PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT

Sustainable procurement indicators may include:

- Percentage of sustainable suppliers.
- Green procurement adoption.
- Local supplier participation.
- Supplier compliance rates.
- Environmental improvements achieved.

---

# ARTICLE XVI

# REPORTING

TJF shall periodically review:

- Procurement sustainability performance.
- Supplier progress.
- Environmental benefits.
- Improvement opportunities.

---

# ARTICLE XVII

# FUTURE SUPPLY CHAIN VISION (2035–2100)

## 2026–2035

Foundation Stage:

- Establish sustainable procurement standards.
- Train procurement teams.
- Introduce supplier assessments.

---

## 2036–2050

Transformation Stage:

- Develop sustainable supplier networks.
- Expand green purchasing.
- Improve supply chain transparency.

---

## 2051–2100

Global Leadership Stage:

- Operate a fully responsible supply ecosystem.
- Support regenerative supply chains.
- Influence sustainable markets worldwide.

---

# ARTICLE XVIII

# FINAL DECLARATION

> "Every procurement decision is an opportunity to create positive change. Through responsible sourcing, ethical partnerships and sustainable choices, The Jopetnah Foundation commits to building supply chains that serve people and protect the planet."
