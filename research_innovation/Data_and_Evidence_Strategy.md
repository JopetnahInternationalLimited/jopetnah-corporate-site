# Data and Evidence Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that quality data and evidence are essential for effective decision-making, accountability, innovation and sustainable impact.

Development challenges are increasingly complex and require organizations to understand realities through reliable information, advanced analysis and continuous learning. TJF therefore commits to developing strong data systems that support programme design, implementation, evaluation and strategic planning.

This Data and Evidence Strategy establishes TJF’s approach to responsible data collection, management, analysis, utilization and sharing.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s data governance approach.
- Strengthen evidence-based decision-making.
- Improve programme monitoring and evaluation.
- Promote data-driven innovation.
- Enhance institutional learning.
- Support transparency and accountability.

---
