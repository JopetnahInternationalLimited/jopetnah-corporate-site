# Environmental Protection Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that healthy ecosystems, clean environments and sustainable natural resources are essential foundations for human wellbeing and long-term development.

Environmental degradation, pollution, unsustainable resource extraction and ecosystem loss threaten communities, economies and future generations. TJF therefore commits to integrating environmental protection principles across its operations, programmes, partnerships and investments.

This Environmental Protection Framework establishes TJF’s approach to preventing environmental harm, protecting natural resources, promoting responsible development and supporting communities in becoming environmental stewards.

---

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s environmental protection standards.
- Prevent environmental degradation.
- Promote responsible resource management.
- Strengthen environmental risk management.
- Support ecosystem conservation.
- Encourage community environmental stewardship.
