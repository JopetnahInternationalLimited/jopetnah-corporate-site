Digital Process and Information Support for an Enterprise Client

Sectors: Technology & Digital Solutions

2023 – 2024

Location: Central Uganda

Client Type: Private enterprise

Challenge

The enterprise faced operational coordination challenges associated with manual workflows and fragmented information management. Selected processes required a more structured approach to recording, accessing and using customer-related and operational information.

Our Role

Jopetnah International Limited assessed selected workflows and supported the consideration and introduction of proportionate digital tools and process improvements. The emphasis was on practical solutions aligned with the enterprise’s operational requirements and capacity, rather than an unnecessarily complex systems overhaul.

Approach

  • Mapped selected operational and customer-related workflows.
  • Reviewed practical options for digital tools and information structures.
  • Supported configuration and implementation activities within the agreed scope.
  • Provided basic user guidance and orientation for nominated personnel.
  • Prepared handover notes to support continuity and future improvements.

Results

  • A clearer digital workflow for agreed operational activities.
  • More structured handling of information for priority tasks.
  • Staff orientation on the agreed tools, procedures and responsibilities.
  • A foundation for incremental improvements as the enterprise’s requirements evolve.
