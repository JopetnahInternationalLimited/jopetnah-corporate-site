# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# FARMER EMPOWERMENT AND CAPACITY BUILDING PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

Farmers are the backbone of food production, rural economies and community resilience.

However, many farmers face challenges including limited access to modern agricultural knowledge, inadequate training, restricted market opportunities, limited technology adoption and insufficient support systems.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that empowering farmers is essential for achieving sustainable food security and inclusive economic development.

Through this Programme, TJF commits to building stronger, more knowledgeable and more resilient farming communities.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Strengthen farmer knowledge and skills.
- Improve agricultural productivity.
- Promote modern farming practices.
- Support farmer organizations.
- Increase access to agricultural opportunities.
- Enhance rural economic empowerment.
