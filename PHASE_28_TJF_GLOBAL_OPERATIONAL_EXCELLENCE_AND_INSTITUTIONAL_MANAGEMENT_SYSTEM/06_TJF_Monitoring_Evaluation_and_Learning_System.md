# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# MONITORING, EVALUATION AND LEARNING SYSTEM

---

# PREAMBLE

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that sustainable impact requires continuous measurement, reflection and improvement.

The Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) System provides the institutional mechanism through which TJF:

- Measures progress.
- Demonstrates impact.
- Learns from experience.
- Improves decision-making.
- Strengthens accountability.

This System ensures that every TJF initiative is guided by evidence, transparency and continuous improvement.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE OF THE MEL SYSTEM

## Section 1: Objectives

The MEL System aims to:

- Establish impact measurement standards.
- Strengthen programme accountability.
- Improve institutional learning.
- Support evidence-based decisions.
- Demonstrate results to stakeholders.

---

# ARTICLE II

# MEL GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

TJF MEL activities shall follow:

## 1. Evidence-Based Management

Decisions shall be informed by:

- Reliable data.
- Research.
- Performance information.

---

## 2. Accountability

TJF shall demonstrate:

- Responsible resource use.
- Programme effectiveness.
- Measurable outcomes.

---

## 3. Learning Culture

TJF shall continuously learn from:

- Successes.
- Challenges.
- Innovation.
- Experience.

---

## 4. Transparency

Results shall be communicated honestly and responsibly.
