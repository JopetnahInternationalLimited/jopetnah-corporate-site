# Data-Driven Impact and Analytics Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes data as a strategic institutional asset that enables better decisions, stronger accountability and measurable impact.

A globally effective institution must understand its performance, learn from evidence and continuously improve its programmes and operations.

This Data-Driven Impact and Analytics Framework establishes TJF’s approach to collecting, managing, analyzing and applying data to achieve sustainable transformation.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s global data strategy.
- Strengthen evidence-based decision-making.
- Improve impact measurement.
- Develop institutional intelligence systems.
- Support continuous improvement.

---

# 2. Data Vision

To become a data-intelligent global institution where information drives innovation, accountability and sustainable impact.

---

# 3. Data Mission

To collect, analyze and apply reliable information to improve TJF’s programmes, operations and strategic decisions.

---

# 4. Data Philosophy

TJF believes:

> "Data transforms information into knowledge, and knowledge enables better decisions."

Effective data management requires:

- Accuracy.
- Security.
- Accessibility.
- Responsible use.
- Continuous learning.
