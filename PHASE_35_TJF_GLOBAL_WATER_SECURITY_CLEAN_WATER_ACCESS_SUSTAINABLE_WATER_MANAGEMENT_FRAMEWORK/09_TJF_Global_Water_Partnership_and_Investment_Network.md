# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# GLOBAL WATER PARTNERSHIP AND INVESTMENT NETWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

Water security is a shared global responsibility requiring collaboration, innovation and sustainable investment.

The scale of global water challenges exceeds the capacity of any single organization or institution. Sustainable solutions require coordinated action among governments, development organizations, financial institutions, businesses, communities and scientific partners.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes strategic partnerships as a foundation for expanding water access, developing resilient infrastructure and accelerating innovation.

Through this Framework, TJF commits to building a global network dedicated to advancing sustainable water security.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Framework shall:

- Establish TJF's global water partnership model.
- Mobilize sustainable water investment.
- Strengthen institutional cooperation.
- Encourage public-private collaboration.
- Support innovative water financing.
- Expand global water security initiatives.
