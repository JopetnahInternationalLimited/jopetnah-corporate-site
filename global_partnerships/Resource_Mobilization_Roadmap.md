# Resource Mobilization Roadmap

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that achieving sustainable social transformation requires a deliberate and strategic approach to mobilizing financial, technical, human and institutional resources.

Resource mobilization extends beyond fundraising. It involves building ecosystems of support that combine funding, partnerships, expertise, innovation, networks and strategic opportunities.

This Resource Mobilization Roadmap establishes TJF’s long-term approach for identifying, acquiring, managing and expanding resources required to deliver impactful programmes worldwide.

The Roadmap provides a structured pathway for transforming relationships into sustainable support systems while maintaining accountability, transparency and institutional excellence.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Roadmap aims to:

- Establish TJF’s resource mobilization direction.
- Create diversified resource pipelines.
- Strengthen fundraising capacity.
- Improve partnership-driven resource acquisition.
- Support long-term institutional sustainability.
- Enable global programme expansion.
