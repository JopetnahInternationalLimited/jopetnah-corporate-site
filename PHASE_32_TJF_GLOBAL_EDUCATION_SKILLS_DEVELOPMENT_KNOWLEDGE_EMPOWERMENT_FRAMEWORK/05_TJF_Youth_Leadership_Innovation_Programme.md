# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# YOUTH LEADERSHIP AND INNOVATION PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

Young people represent the creativity, energy and future leadership capacity of every society.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that many young people face challenges including limited access to mentorship, skills development, innovation opportunities and pathways toward meaningful participation in economic and social development.

Through this Programme, TJF commits to building a generation of ethical, innovative and empowered young leaders capable of creating positive change locally and globally.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Develop future leaders.
- Promote youth innovation.
- Encourage entrepreneurship.
- Strengthen leadership capabilities.
- Create youth opportunity networks.
- Support community transformation.
