# Innovation Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes innovation as a powerful driver of sustainable development, social transformation and improved community outcomes.

Complex global challenges require new ideas, technologies, partnerships and approaches that go beyond traditional solutions. TJF therefore promotes innovation as a core institutional capability for designing, testing and scaling solutions that create meaningful impact.

This Innovation Strategy establishes TJF’s framework for advancing social innovation, technological innovation, research-driven solutions and entrepreneurial approaches across its programmes and partnerships.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s innovation ecosystem.
- Promote creative problem-solving.
- Support technology-driven solutions.
- Encourage experimentation and learning.
- Scale impactful innovations.
- Strengthen innovation partnerships.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to:

- TJF programmes.
- Research activities.
- Community initiatives.
- Digital solutions.
- Entrepreneurship programmes.
- Partnerships.
- Internal operations.

# 3. Innovation Vision

To become a globally recognized innovation-driven foundation that develops scalable solutions for sustainable development challenges.

---

# 4. Innovation Mission

To empower individuals, communities and organizations to create, test and implement innovative solutions that improve lives.

# 5. Innovation Principles

TJF innovation initiatives shall follow:

## Impact Driven

Innovation must solve meaningful problems and create measurable benefits.

## Human-Centered

---

## Collaboration

Innovation grows through partnerships and shared knowledge.

---

## Experimentation

Testing, learning and improvement are essential.

---

## Scalability

Successful solutions should have potential for expansion.

---

## Sustainability

Innovations should create lasting value.
