# Corporate Partnership and CSR Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes the private sector as a powerful driver of innovation, economic growth, job creation and sustainable development.

Businesses increasingly seek meaningful ways to contribute to society through Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR), Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) commitments, sustainability programmes and impact investments.

This Corporate Partnership and CSR Framework establishes TJF’s approach for developing strategic collaborations with corporations, businesses and investors to create shared value and measurable social impact.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s corporate partnership model.
- Strengthen private sector collaboration.
- Support CSR and ESG initiatives.
- Mobilize corporate resources and expertise.
- Develop sustainable impact partnerships.
- Promote responsible business engagement.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to partnerships with:

- Multinational corporations.
- Local businesses.
- SMEs.
- Financial institutions.
- Technology companies.
- Manufacturing organizations.
- Investors.
- Business associations.

---

# 3. Corporate Partnership Vision

To become a trusted strategic partner for businesses seeking to create measurable social, environmental and economic impact.

---

# 4. Corporate Partnership Mission

To connect private sector innovation, resources and expertise with community-focused development initiatives that create shared value.

---

# 5. Partnership Principles

Corporate partnerships shall follow:

## Shared Value Creation

Partnerships should benefit businesses, communities and society.

---

## Sustainability

Initiatives should create lasting outcomes.

---

## Transparency

Corporate collaborations shall operate openly and ethically.

---

## Accountability

Resources and commitments shall be managed responsibly.

---

## Innovation

Partnerships should encourage new solutions.

---

## Impact Measurement

Results should be measurable and evidence-based.
