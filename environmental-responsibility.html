Policy Statement

Our Commitment

Responsible environmental stewardship is part of how we plan, procure and deliver our work.

Guiding principle: Responsible business growth should be pursued with due regard for the environment and the communities in which JIL operates.

Environment & Resource Management

Purpose

Jopetnah International Limited (JIL) is committed to responsible environmental stewardship across its operations, projects, procurement activities and business partnerships. This policy establishes the principles through which the company seeks to prevent avoidable environmental harm, use resources responsibly and integrate environmental considerations into business decisions.

Environment & Resource Management

Scope

This policy applies to JIL employees, management, project teams and other personnel acting on the company’s behalf. Suppliers, contractors and business partners are encouraged to apply equivalent environmental standards, proportionate to the nature, scale and risks of their engagements with JIL.

Environment & Resource Management

Policy Commitments

  • Comply with applicable environmental laws, regulations, permits and relevant contractual requirements.
  • Identify and manage material environmental risks associated with activities under JIL’s control.
  • Promote the efficient use of energy, water, raw materials and other resources.
  • Consider lower-impact products, services, technologies and operating methods where practical and commercially reasonable.
  • Take reasonable measures to prevent pollution, avoid unnecessary environmental damage and manage waste responsibly.
  • Encourage suppliers and partners to adopt environmentally responsible practices.
  • Consider environmental risks and opportunities during project planning, procurement and implementation.
  • Review environmental performance and identify opportunities for continuous improvement.
Environment & Resource Management

Implementation

Environmental considerations will be incorporated into relevant operational and project decisions according to the nature and risk of each engagement. Where specialist assessments, permits or technical expertise are required, JIL will seek appropriately qualified support.

Environment & Resource Management

Responsibilities

Management is responsible for promoting implementation of this policy and providing appropriate oversight. Employees and project teams are expected to follow relevant environmental requirements and report significant concerns. Project leads will address environmental risks relevant to their assignments.

Environment & Resource Management

Monitoring and Review

Where proportionate, JIL will maintain records of significant environmental risks, incidents, corrective actions and improvement initiatives. This policy will be reviewed at least annually and when material legal, operational or business changes warrant an earlier review.

Governance

Accountability & Related Policies

Management is responsible for ensuring that this document is communicated to relevant personnel and reviewed when necessary. Implementation should be proportionate to the nature and scale of JIL’s activities and consistent with applicable legal, contractual and site-specific requirements.

Read this document alongside the company’s relevant governance, risk management, procurement, health and safety, ethics, privacy and sustainability materials, as applicable.
