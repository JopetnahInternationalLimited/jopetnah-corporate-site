# Asset and Resource Management Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that effective management of institutional assets and resources is essential for achieving sustainable impact.

Assets represent more than physical possessions; they include financial resources, intellectual property, technology, human capabilities, partnerships, knowledge systems and institutional reputation.

This Asset and Resource Management Framework establishes TJF’s approach for acquiring, protecting, utilizing, maintaining and optimizing resources to support its mission and long-term sustainability.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish effective asset management systems.
- Protect institutional resources.
- Improve resource utilization.
- Strengthen operational efficiency.
- Support accountability.
- Maximize social value creation.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Physical assets.
- Financial assets.
- Digital assets.
- Intellectual property.
- Human resources.
- Knowledge resources.
- Strategic partnerships.
- Institutional reputation.

---

# 3. Asset Management Vision

To build a world-class resource management system that preserves, optimizes and transforms institutional assets into sustainable impact.

---

# 4. Asset Management Mission

To ensure that all TJF resources are responsibly acquired, managed and utilized for maximum organizational and community benefit.

---

# 5. Asset Management Principles

TJF shall follow:

## Stewardship

Resources shall be managed as entrusted assets.

---

## Accountability

All assets shall have clear ownership and responsibility.

---

## Efficiency

Resources shall generate maximum value.

---

## Protection

Assets shall be safeguarded against misuse or loss.

---

## Sustainability

Resources shall support long-term objectives.

---

## Transparency

Asset decisions shall be properly documented.

---

# 6. Strategic Objectives

TJF seeks to:

- Establish comprehensive asset registers.
- Improve resource planning.
- Reduce asset losses.
- Optimize operational efficiency.
- Protect institutional knowledge.
- Increase asset contribution to mission delivery.
