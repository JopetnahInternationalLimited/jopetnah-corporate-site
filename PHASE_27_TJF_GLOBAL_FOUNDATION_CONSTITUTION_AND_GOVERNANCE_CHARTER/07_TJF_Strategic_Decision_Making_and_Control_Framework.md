# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# STRATEGIC DECISION-MAKING AND CONTROL FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that effective decision-making is essential for achieving sustainable institutional impact.

As TJF grows into a global legacy institution, decisions must be guided by:

- Clear authority.
- Strategic alignment.
- Evidence-based analysis.
- Responsible oversight.
- Institutional accountability.

This Framework establishes the systems through which TJF makes, reviews and controls strategic decisions.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE OF THE FRAMEWORK

## Section 1: Objectives

This Framework aims to:

- Establish decision-making principles.
- Define approval authorities.
- Strengthen institutional controls.
- Prevent governance failures.
- Promote responsible leadership.

---

# ARTICLE II

# DECISION-MAKING PRINCIPLES

All TJF decisions shall follow these principles:

## 1. Mission Alignment

Every decision must support TJF’s purpose and values.

---

## 2. Evidence-Based Judgment

Decisions should be supported by:

- Research.
- Data.
- Analysis.
- Professional expertise.

---

## 3. Accountability

Every major decision must have:

- A responsible authority.
- Clear documentation.
- Measurable outcomes.

---

## 4. Long-Term Thinking

Decisions must consider:

- Present impact.
- Future consequences.
- Institutional legacy.

---

# ARTICLE III

# STRATEGIC AUTHORITY STRUCTURE

The decision hierarchy shall follow:

```
                 TJF CONSTITUTION

                        ↓

              BOARD OF TRUSTEES

                        ↓

          EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM

                        ↓

              MANAGEMENT LEVELS

                        ↓

            PROGRAMME IMPLEMENTATION
```

---

# ARTICLE IV

# BOARD STRATEGIC AUTHORITY

The Board of Trustees shall approve:

- Institutional strategy.
- Long-term priorities.
- Major investments.
- Significant partnerships.
- Organisational restructuring.
- Constitutional matters.

---

# ARTICLE V

# EXECUTIVE DECISION AUTHORITY

Executive leadership shall:

- Implement approved strategy.
- Manage operations.
- Allocate approved resources.
- Recommend strategic initiatives.
- Respond to operational needs.

---

# ARTICLE VI

# MANAGEMENT DECISION AUTHORITY

Management teams shall:

- Execute approved plans.
- Manage departments.
- Coordinate activities.
- Monitor performance.
- Report operational results.

---

# ARTICLE VII

# DECISION CLASSIFICATION MODEL

TJF decisions shall be classified as:

## Level 1 — Constitutional Decisions

Examples:

- Mission changes.
- Governance amendments.
- Institutional identity matters.

Authority:

Board of Trustees.

---

## Level 2 — Strategic Decisions

Examples:

- New global initiatives.
- Major investments.
- Expansion strategies.

Authority:

Board approval with executive implementation.

---

## Level 3 — Operational Decisions

Examples:

- Programme activities.
- Department actions.
- Routine administration.

Authority:

Executive and management teams.

---

## Level 4 — Administrative Decisions

Examples:

- Daily operational matters.
- Internal coordination.

Authority:

Assigned managers.

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# STRATEGIC REVIEW PROCESS

Major decisions shall follow:

```
Identify Opportunity

        ↓

Conduct Analysis

        ↓

Assess Risks

        ↓

Develop Recommendation

        ↓

Approve Decision

        ↓

Implement Action

        ↓

Measure Results
```

---

# ARTICLE IX

# CONTROL FRAMEWORK

TJF shall maintain institutional controls through:

## Governance Controls

Ensuring:

- Proper authority.
- Oversight.
- Accountability.

---

## Financial Controls

Ensuring:

- Responsible resource use.
- Budget discipline.
- Asset protection.

---

## Operational Controls

Ensuring:

- Effective processes.
- Quality delivery.
- Performance management.

---

## Ethical Controls

Ensuring:

- Integrity.
- Compliance.
- Responsible conduct.

---

# ARTICLE X

# DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY

Authority may be delegated to appropriate levels while maintaining accountability.

Delegation shall include:

- Clear responsibilities.
- Defined limits.
- Reporting requirements.
- Performance expectations.

Delegation does not remove ultimate accountability.

---

# ARTICLE XI

# CHECKS AND BALANCES SYSTEM

TJF shall maintain separation between:

## Decision Authority

Who approves decisions.

↓

## Implementation Authority

Who executes decisions.

↓

## Review Authority

Who evaluates results.

---

# ARTICLE XII

# STRATEGIC PERFORMANCE MONITORING

Strategic decisions shall be evaluated through:

- Key performance indicators.
- Programme outcomes.
- Financial performance.
- Institutional impact measurements.

---

# ARTICLE XIII

# RISK-BASED DECISION MAKING

Before major decisions, TJF shall evaluate:

- Financial risks.
- Operational risks.
- Reputation risks.
- Legal risks.
- Strategic risks.

---

# ARTICLE XIV

# CRISIS DECISION FRAMEWORK

During emergencies:

```
Crisis Identification

        ↓

Emergency Assessment

        ↓

Leadership Coordination

        ↓

Rapid Decision

        ↓

Recovery Review
```

Emergency decisions shall still respect TJF values and governance principles.

---

# ARTICLE XV

# DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

Major decisions shall include:

- Decision records.
- Supporting analysis.
- Approval documentation.
- Implementation plans.
- Review outcomes.

---

# ARTICLE XVI

# CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

TJF shall regularly review:

- Decision effectiveness.
- Governance processes.
- Control systems.
- Institutional learning.

---

# ARTICLE XVII

# LEGACY DECISION PRINCIPLE

Every major decision shall consider:

- Will this strengthen TJF?
- Will this protect future generations?
- Will this advance the mission?
- Will this preserve institutional trust?

---

# FINAL DECISION-MAKING DECLARATION

> "At The Jopetnah Foundation, authority exists to serve the mission, and decisions exist to create sustainable impact."
