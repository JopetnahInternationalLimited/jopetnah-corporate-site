# Climate Resilience and Adaptation Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that climate change presents increasing risks to communities, ecosystems, economies and development progress.

Climate-related impacts including floods, droughts, extreme weather events, food insecurity, water shortages and ecosystem degradation disproportionately affect vulnerable populations. TJF therefore commits to strengthening climate resilience through adaptation planning, community empowerment, sustainable livelihoods and resilient development systems.

This Climate Resilience and Adaptation Framework establishes TJF’s approach to helping communities anticipate, prepare for, respond to and recover from climate-related challenges.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s climate adaptation approach.
- Strengthen community resilience.
- Reduce climate-related risks.
- Support sustainable livelihoods.
- Promote climate-resilient development.
- Integrate adaptation into programmes.
