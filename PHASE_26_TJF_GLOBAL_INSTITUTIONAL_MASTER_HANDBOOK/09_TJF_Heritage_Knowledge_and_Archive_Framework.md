# TJF Heritage, Knowledge and Archive Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes institutional knowledge as one of its most valuable long-term assets.

This Heritage, Knowledge and Archive Framework establishes the systems required to capture, preserve, organize and transfer TJF’s institutional knowledge, history, achievements and intellectual contributions.

The framework ensures that TJF maintains continuity beyond individuals, leadership transitions and changing environments.

It protects:

- Institutional history.
- Strategic knowledge.
- Programme experiences.
- Research outputs.
- Leadership wisdom.
- Global achievements.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Preserve TJF institutional heritage.
- Establish knowledge management systems.
- Create digital and physical archives.
- Protect institutional memory.
- Support future generations of leaders.

---

# 2. Heritage Vision

> "To preserve the story, knowledge and achievements of TJF as a lasting institutional legacy for generations worldwide."

---

# 3. Heritage Mission

To document, protect and share TJF’s institutional knowledge, experiences and contributions through structured archives, research systems and learning platforms.

---

# 4. Knowledge Philosophy

TJF believes:

> "Knowledge created through experience becomes a legacy when it is preserved, shared and used to inspire future progress."

Institutional knowledge must be:

- Captured.
- Organized.
- Protected.
- Shared.
- Continuously improved.
