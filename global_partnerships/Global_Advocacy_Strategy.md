# Global Advocacy Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes advocacy as a powerful tool for advancing sustainable development, influencing positive change and ensuring that community voices are represented in national and global conversations.

Advocacy enables organizations to move beyond programme implementation by addressing root causes, influencing policies, promoting awareness and encouraging collective action.

This Global Advocacy Strategy establishes TJF’s framework for developing evidence-based advocacy initiatives that promote inclusive development, social justice, economic empowerment and sustainable transformation.

The Strategy positions TJF as a credible contributor to global development discussions while maintaining neutrality, professionalism and commitment to community-centered solutions.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s global advocacy approach.
- Promote awareness of critical development challenges.
- Amplify community voices.
- Influence positive policy outcomes.
- Strengthen TJF’s global visibility.
- Support sustainable development efforts.

# 5. Advocacy Principles

TJF advocacy shall be guided by:

## Evidence-Based Approach

Advocacy positions shall be informed by research, data and community experience.

---

## Community-Centered Advocacy

The voices and experiences of affected communities shall remain central.

---

## Independence

TJF shall maintain professional independence and integrity.

---

## Constructive Engagement

Advocacy shall focus on solutions and collaboration.

---

## Inclusion

Advocacy shall promote participation of diverse communities.

---

## Accountability

Advocacy commitments shall be transparent and measurable.
