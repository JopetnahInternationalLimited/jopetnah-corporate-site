Capacity Building Programme for Agricultural Cooperatives

Sectors: Agricultural Development & Agribusiness; Training & Capacity Building

2024 – 2025

Location: Eastern Uganda

Client Type: Development partner / Cooperative support programme

Challenge

Several agricultural cooperatives needed practical support to strengthen internal management, improve record-keeping, and enhance market access for their members.

Our Role

Jopetnah designed and delivered a structured capacity-building programme focused on cooperative governance, basic financial management, and market-oriented planning.

Approach

  • Needs assessment with cooperative leaders
  • Customised training modules
  • Practical workshops and mentoring sessions
  • Simple tools for record-keeping and planning

Results

  • Improved governance practices among participating cooperatives
  • Better financial record-keeping systems introduced
  • Strengthened capacity of cooperative leaders
  • Clearer pathways for market engagement identified

Note: Client name and specific figures have been withheld for confidentiality. Additional case studies will be published progressively.
