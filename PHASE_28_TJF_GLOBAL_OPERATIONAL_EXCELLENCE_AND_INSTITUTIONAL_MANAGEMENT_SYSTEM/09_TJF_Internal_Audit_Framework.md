# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# INTERNAL AUDIT FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

The Jopetnah Foundation is committed to maintaining the highest standards of:

- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Integrity.
- Operational excellence.
- Responsible stewardship.

The Internal Audit Framework provides independent assurance that TJF systems, processes and controls operate effectively and support the Foundation’s mission.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE OF THE INTERNAL AUDIT FRAMEWORK

## Section 1: Objectives

This Framework aims to:

- Strengthen governance.
- Improve internal controls.
- Protect institutional resources.
- Identify improvement opportunities.
- Support effective decision-making.

---

# ARTICLE II

# INTERNAL AUDIT PHILOSOPHY

TJF believes:

> "Internal audit is not only a control function; it is a strategic partner that helps the institution improve and grow."

Internal audit shall focus on:

- Assurance.
- Improvement.
- Risk management.
- Compliance.
- Institutional learning.

---

# ARTICLE III

# INTERNAL AUDIT GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

The audit structure shall follow:

```
                 BOARD OF TRUSTEES

                         ↓

              AUDIT AND RISK COMMITTEE

                         ↓

             CHIEF INTERNAL AUDITOR

                         ↓

              INTERNAL AUDIT TEAM

                         ↓

       DEPARTMENTS AND PROGRAMME UNITS
```

---

# ARTICLE IV

# PRINCIPLES OF INTERNAL AUDITING

## 1. Independence

Internal audit shall operate objectively and independently.

---

## 2. Professional Integrity

Auditors shall demonstrate:

- Honesty.
- Confidentiality.
- Professional judgement.

---

## 3. Evidence-Based Assessment

Audit conclusions shall be based on:

- Verified information.
- Documentation.
- Reliable evidence.

---

## 4. Improvement Orientation

Audits shall identify opportunities to strengthen TJF systems.

---

# ARTICLE V

# INTERNAL AUDIT FUNCTIONS

The Internal Audit Department shall evaluate:

## 1. Governance Effectiveness

Reviewing:

- Decision-making processes.
- Accountability structures.
- Policy implementation.

---

## 2. Financial Management

Reviewing:

- Financial controls.
- Budget management.
- Asset protection.
- Reporting accuracy.

---

## 3. Operational Effectiveness

Reviewing:

- Processes.
- Efficiency.
- Resource utilisation.

---

## 4. Compliance Management

Reviewing:

- Laws.
- Regulations.
- Institutional policies.

---

## 5. Risk Management

Reviewing:

- Risk identification.
- Risk controls.
- Risk response systems.

---

# ARTICLE VI

# INTERNAL AUDIT PROCESS

The audit process shall follow:

```
Audit Planning

       ↓

Risk Assessment

       ↓

Audit Fieldwork

       ↓

Evidence Review

       ↓

Audit Findings

       ↓

Management Response

       ↓

Follow-Up Review
```

---

# ARTICLE VII

# ANNUAL AUDIT PLANNING

The Internal Audit Department shall prepare an annual audit plan considering:

- Institutional risks.
- Strategic priorities.
- Previous audit findings.
- Operational changes.

Priority shall be given to high-risk areas.

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# TYPES OF INTERNAL AUDITS

TJF may conduct:

---

# Financial Audits

Reviewing:

- Financial transactions.
- Budget controls.
- Accounting systems.

---

# Operational Audits

Reviewing:

- Efficiency.
- Procedures.
- Service delivery.

---

# Compliance Audits

Reviewing:

- Regulatory obligations.
- Internal policies.
- Agreements.

---

# Programme Audits

Reviewing:

- Programme implementation.
- Impact systems.
- Resource management.

---

# Technology Audits

Reviewing:

- Digital systems.
- Data security.
- Technology controls.

---

# ARTICLE IX

# AUDIT FINDINGS MANAGEMENT

Audit findings shall be classified according to:

## Critical Findings

Issues requiring immediate action.

---

## High Priority Findings

Issues requiring urgent improvement.

---

## Medium Priority Findings

Issues requiring planned corrective action.

---

## Low Priority Findings

Opportunities for improvement.

---

# ARTICLE X

# MANAGEMENT RESPONSE PROCESS

Management shall provide:

- Explanation of findings.
- Corrective action plans.
- Responsible persons.
- Completion timelines.

---

# ARTICLE XI

# AUDIT FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM

Internal Audit shall monitor:

- Implementation of recommendations.
- Improvement progress.
- Remaining risks.

Follow-up reports shall be presented to leadership.

---

# ARTICLE XII

# FRAUD AND INVESTIGATION SUPPORT

Internal Audit may support investigations relating to:

- Fraud.
- Misuse of resources.
- Financial irregularities.
- Ethical concerns.

Investigations shall follow:

- Confidentiality.
- Fairness.
- Evidence-based procedures.

---

# ARTICLE XIII

# INTERNAL CONTROL FRAMEWORK

TJF shall maintain controls relating to:

## Financial Controls

Including:

- Approval procedures.
- Segregation of duties.
- Financial reviews.

---

## Operational Controls

Including:

- Standard procedures.
- Documentation.
- Performance monitoring.

---

## Information Controls

Including:

- Data security.
- Access management.
- Confidentiality.

---

# ARTICLE XIV

# AUDIT REPORTING STRUCTURE

Internal Audit shall provide:

## Audit Reports

Containing:

- Scope.
- Findings.
- Recommendations.
- Management responses.

---

## Quarterly Reports

Containing:

- Audit progress.
- Major findings.
- Risk observations.

---

## Annual Audit Report

Containing:

- Overall control assessment.
- Institutional improvement recommendations.

---

# ARTICLE XV

# AUDIT QUALITY ASSURANCE

The Internal Audit Department shall maintain quality through:

- Professional standards.
- Staff training.
- Periodic reviews.
- Continuous improvement.

---

# ARTICLE XVI

# RELATIONSHIP WITH EXTERNAL AUDITORS

TJF Internal Audit shall cooperate with:

- External auditors.
- Regulators.
- Funding partners.

while maintaining its independence.

---

# ARTICLE XVII

# AUDIT CULTURE DEVELOPMENT

TJF shall promote a culture where audits are viewed as:

- Improvement opportunities.
- Learning mechanisms.
- Institutional strengthening tools.

---

# ARTICLE XVIII

# FUTURE INTERNAL AUDIT VISION

By 2035–2100, TJF shall maintain an internal audit function recognised for:

- Independence.
- Professional excellence.
- Strategic value.
- Strong institutional assurance.

---

# FINAL INTERNAL AUDIT DECLARATION

> "Internal audit protects the integrity of today’s operations while strengthening the institution’s ability to achieve tomorrow’s vision."
