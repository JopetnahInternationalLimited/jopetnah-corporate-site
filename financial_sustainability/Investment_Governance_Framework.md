# Investment Governance Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that responsible investment governance is essential for protecting institutional resources, achieving sustainable financial growth and ensuring that investments remain aligned with the Foundation’s mission.

As TJF develops investment activities through endowment funds, impact investments, social enterprises and strategic financial instruments, strong governance systems are required to ensure transparency, accountability, ethical decision-making and effective risk management.

This Investment Governance Framework establishes TJF’s structure, policies and processes for managing investment activities professionally and responsibly.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s investment governance system.
- Define investment responsibilities.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Protect institutional capital.
- Support informed investment decisions.
- Ensure mission-aligned investment practices.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Endowment investments.
- Impact investments.
- Financial assets.
- Strategic investments.
- Social enterprise investments.
- Investment partnerships.
- Institutional reserves.

---

# 3. Investment Governance Vision

To establish a world-class investment governance system that preserves capital, generates sustainable returns and advances TJF’s long-term mission.

---

# 4. Investment Governance Mission

To manage TJF investments through disciplined, transparent and responsible processes that balance financial performance with social impact.

---

# 5. Governance Principles

TJF investment governance shall follow:

## Fiduciary Responsibility

All investment decisions must protect TJF resources.

---

## Transparency

Investment activities must be properly documented and reported.

---

## Accountability

Clear responsibilities must exist at every decision level.

---

## Professional Management

Investment decisions should be guided by expertise and evidence.

---

## Risk Awareness

Investment risks must be identified and managed.

---

## Mission Alignment

Investments should support TJF values and objectives.

---

# 6. Strategic Objectives

TJF seeks to:

- Establish professional investment structures.
- Improve investment decision-making.
- Protect financial resources.
- Generate sustainable returns.
- Support impact-oriented investments.
- Maintain stakeholder confidence.

---

# 7. Investment Governance Structure

The TJF investment governance model:

```
Board of Directors
          ↓
Investment Committee
          ↓
Executive Director
          ↓
Investment Management Team
          ↓
Finance Department
          ↓
External Advisors & Partners
```

---

# 8. Board of Directors

The Board provides ultimate oversight responsibility.

Responsibilities:

- Approve investment policies.
- Approve major investments.
- Ensure fiduciary responsibility.
- Monitor investment performance.
- Ensure alignment with TJF mission.

---

# 9. Investment Committee

The Investment Committee serves as the primary investment oversight body.

Responsibilities:

- Develop investment recommendations.
- Review investment opportunities.
- Monitor portfolio performance.
- Assess investment risks.
- Recommend strategic adjustments.

Membership may include:

- Board representatives.
- Finance professionals.
- Investment experts.
- Technical advisors.

---

# 10. Executive Director

The Executive Director is responsible for:

- Implementing investment strategy.
- Ensuring operational compliance.
- Coordinating investment activities.
- Reporting performance to leadership.

---

# 11. Investment Management Team

Responsibilities:

- Conduct investment research.
- Evaluate opportunities.
- Prepare investment proposals.
- Monitor investments.
- Maintain investment records.

---

# 12. Finance Department

Responsibilities:

- Maintain financial records.
- Support investment reporting.
- Ensure compliance.
- Monitor cash flows.
- Coordinate audits.

---

# 13. External Investment Advisors

Where appropriate, TJF may engage:

- Investment advisors.
- Financial analysts.
- Legal experts.
- Technical specialists.

Responsibilities:

- Provide market expertise.
- Support investment analysis.
- Offer independent advice.

---

# 14. Investment Decision Process

TJF shall follow:

```
Investment Opportunity Identification
              ↓
Preliminary Assessment
              ↓
Due Diligence
              ↓
Impact Evaluation
              ↓
Financial Analysis
              ↓
Investment Committee Review
              ↓
Board Approval (where required)
              ↓
Investment Implementation
              ↓
Performance Monitoring
```

---

# 15. Investment Due Diligence Framework

Before approval, investments shall be assessed based on:

## Financial Assessment

Including:

- Revenue model.
- Financial projections.
- Expected returns.
- Liquidity considerations.

---

## Operational Assessment

Including:

- Management capability.
- Business model.
- Operational systems.

---

## Risk Assessment

Including:

- Market risks.
- Regulatory risks.
- Financial risks.

---

## Impact Assessment

Including:

- Social outcomes.
- Environmental considerations.
- Community benefits.

---

# 16. Investment Policy Statement (IPS)

TJF shall maintain an Investment Policy Statement defining:

- Investment objectives.
- Risk tolerance.
- Asset allocation.
- Investment restrictions.
- Performance expectations.
- Reporting requirements.

---

# 17. Ethical Investment Standards

TJF investments should avoid activities involving:

- Human exploitation.
- Environmental destruction.
- Corruption.
- Illegal activities.
- Practices inconsistent with TJF values.

---

# 18. Portfolio Management Framework

TJF shall maintain:

## Diversification

Across:

- Sectors.
- Regions.
- Investment types.

---

## Performance Monitoring

Tracking:

- Financial returns.
- Risk exposure.
- Social impact outcomes.

---

## Portfolio Review

Conducted:

- Regularly.
- During strategic reviews.
- Following market changes.

---

# 19. Investment Reporting Framework

Reports shall include:

## Financial Performance

Including:

- Returns achieved.
- Portfolio value.
- Investment growth.

---

## Impact Performance

Including:

- Beneficiaries reached.
- Development outcomes.
- Sustainability achievements.

---

## Risk Reporting

Including:

- Emerging risks.
- Mitigation actions.
- Portfolio exposure.

---

# 20. Risk Management

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Investment loss | Due diligence and diversification |
| Poor decision-making | Governance oversight |
| Market volatility | Long-term investment approach |
| Conflict of interest | Disclosure requirements |
| Mission drift | Impact alignment reviews |

---

# 21. Conflict of Interest Management

All investment decision-makers shall:

- Declare potential conflicts.
- Avoid improper influence.
- Maintain professional independence.
- Follow ethical standards.

---
