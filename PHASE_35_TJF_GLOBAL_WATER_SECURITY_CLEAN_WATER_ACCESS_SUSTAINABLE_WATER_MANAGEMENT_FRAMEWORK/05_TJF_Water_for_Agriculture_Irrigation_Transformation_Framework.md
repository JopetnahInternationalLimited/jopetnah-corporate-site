# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# WATER FOR AGRICULTURE AND IRRIGATION TRANSFORMATION FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

Agriculture remains one of the largest users of freshwater resources and plays a critical role in global food security, rural livelihoods and economic development.

However, unpredictable rainfall patterns, droughts, water scarcity and inefficient irrigation practices continue to threaten agricultural productivity.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that sustainable irrigation and responsible agricultural water management are essential for building resilient food systems.

Through this Framework, TJF commits to supporting farmers, agricultural enterprises and communities with innovative water solutions that increase productivity while protecting natural resources.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Framework shall:

- Promote sustainable agricultural water management.
- Expand irrigation opportunities.
- Improve farming productivity.
