# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# YOUTH FINANCIAL INCLUSION AND ECONOMIC LITERACY PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

Financial knowledge is a foundation for economic independence, personal stability and sustainable development.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that many young people face financial challenges due to limited access to financial education, inadequate understanding of financial systems, limited investment awareness and barriers to formal financial services.

Through this Programme, TJF commits to building financially empowered generations capable of making informed decisions, creating wealth responsibly and contributing to economic growth.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Improve youth financial literacy.
- Promote responsible financial behaviour.
- Increase access to financial services.
- Encourage savings and investment.
- Support wealth creation.
- Strengthen economic independence.

---

# ARTICLE II

# FINANCIAL EMPOWERMENT VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To develop financially confident young people who can manage resources wisely, access economic opportunities and create sustainable prosperity."
