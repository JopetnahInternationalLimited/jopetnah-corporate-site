Corporate Governance

Governance & Leadership

Sound governance, clear accountability and ethical leadership form the foundation of how Jopetnah International Limited operates and grows.

Principles

Our Governance Approach

We are committed to operating with integrity, transparency and accountability. Our governance framework is designed to be practical and appropriate to our current size while providing a solid foundation for responsible growth.

  • Clear lines of responsibility and decision-making
  • Ethical conduct in all business dealings
  • Compliance with applicable Ugandan laws and regulations
  • Progressive development of formal policies and controls
  • Accountability to clients, partners, employees and other stakeholders
Oversight

Board of Directors

The Board (or equivalent oversight body) provides strategic direction, oversight and accountability. It is responsible for ensuring the organisation operates with integrity, manages key risks, and pursues sustainable growth.
