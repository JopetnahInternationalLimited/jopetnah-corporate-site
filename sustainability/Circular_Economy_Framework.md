# Circular Economy Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable development requires a transition from a linear economic model of "take, use and dispose" toward a circular economy approach that minimizes waste, maximizes resource efficiency and creates lasting economic and environmental value.

The circular economy promotes responsible production and consumption by keeping materials in use for as long as possible, recovering valuable resources and designing systems that regenerate natural resources.

This Circular Economy Framework establishes TJF’s commitment to promoting waste reduction, resource efficiency, sustainable production, recycling innovation and community-based circular solutions.

# 5. Circular Economy Principles

TJF circular economy initiatives shall follow:

## Reduce

Minimize unnecessary consumption and waste generation.

---

## Reuse

Extend the lifespan of products and materials.

---

## Repair

Promote maintenance and restoration instead of replacement.

---

## Recover

Extract value from materials that would otherwise become waste.

---

## Regenerate

Support restoration of natural systems.

# 7. Circular Economy Model

TJF promotes the following transition:

```
Traditional Linear Economy

Extract
  ↓
Produce
  ↓
Consume
  ↓
Dispose


Circular Economy

Design Sustainably
        ↓
Reduce Consumption
        ↓
Reuse Materials
        ↓
Repair Products
        ↓
Recycle Resources
        ↓
Regenerate Systems
```
