# Artificial Intelligence and Automation Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes Artificial Intelligence (AI) and automation as powerful tools for improving institutional performance, strengthening programme delivery and creating innovative solutions.

Responsible adoption of AI will enable TJF to analyze information faster, improve operational efficiency, personalize services and expand its ability to create sustainable impact.

This Artificial Intelligence and Automation Strategy establishes the principles, governance systems and implementation roadmap for integrating AI into TJF’s operations.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s AI adoption framework.
- Promote responsible AI usage.
- Improve institutional efficiency.
- Strengthen data-driven decisions.
- Support innovation and transformation.

---

# 2. AI Vision

To become a responsible AI-enabled global institution that uses advanced technology to improve human development outcomes.

---

# 3. AI Mission

To integrate artificial intelligence and automation ethically and strategically to enhance TJF’s programmes, operations, research and global impact.

---

# 4. AI Philosophy

TJF believes:

> "Technology should strengthen human potential, not replace human responsibility."

AI adoption shall be guided by:

- Ethics.
- Transparency.
- Human oversight.
- Security.
- Social value.
