# Global Campaign Management Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes strategic campaigns as powerful instruments for increasing awareness, influencing behaviour, mobilizing resources and creating collective action around important social development priorities.

A well-designed campaign connects purpose, people, communication and measurable outcomes. Through coordinated campaigns, TJF can amplify community voices, strengthen partnerships, attract supporters and accelerate sustainable development impact.

This Global Campaign Management Framework establishes TJF’s approach for planning, implementing, managing and evaluating campaigns across local, regional and international platforms.

The Framework ensures that every TJF campaign is strategic, audience-focused, measurable and aligned with the Foundation’s mission.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s campaign management standards.
- Create consistent campaign processes.
- Strengthen awareness and engagement.
- Support fundraising and advocacy initiatives.
- Improve campaign effectiveness.
- Build sustainable global movements.
