# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# MATERNAL AND CHILD HEALTH PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

The health of mothers and children determines the strength and sustainability of future generations.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that many families worldwide continue to experience challenges including limited maternal healthcare access, inadequate nutrition, preventable childhood illnesses and barriers to essential health information.

Through this Programme, TJF commits to supporting mothers, newborns and children through accessible, preventive and sustainable healthcare solutions.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Improve maternal healthcare access.
- Support safe pregnancy and childbirth.
- Promote newborn survival.
- Strengthen child healthcare systems.
- Improve nutrition and early development.
- Empower families through health education.

---

# ARTICLE II

# MATERNAL AND CHILD HEALTH VISION

## Vision Statement

> "A future where every mother receives quality care, every child has the opportunity to thrive and every family has access to the support needed for healthy development."

# ARTICLE III

# GUIDING PRINCIPLES

TJF maternal and child health programmes shall follow:

## 1. Protection of Life

Every mother and child deserves access to quality healthcare.

## 2. Early Intervention

Prevention and timely care save lives.

---

## 3. Family-Centred Care

Families are essential partners in health.

---

## 4. Equity

---

## 5. Child Development

## 6. Sustainability

Healthcare solutions must create lasting benefits.
