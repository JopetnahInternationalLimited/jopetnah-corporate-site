# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# HUMAN RESOURCES GOVERNANCE MANUAL

---

# PREAMBLE

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that its greatest strength is its people.

Employees, volunteers, leaders and partners collectively contribute to achieving TJF’s mission of empowerment, development and sustainable impact.

This Human Resources Governance Manual establishes the principles and systems required to create a professional, inclusive and high-performing institutional culture.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE OF THE HUMAN RESOURCES GOVERNANCE MANUAL

## Section 1: Objectives

This Manual aims to:

- Establish people management standards.
- Promote professional excellence.
- Develop institutional talent.
- Protect employee rights.
- Strengthen organisational culture.

---

# ARTICLE II

# TJF HUMAN CAPITAL PHILOSOPHY

TJF believes that:

> "People are not only resources of the institution; they are the builders of its mission and guardians of its legacy."

Human resource management shall focus on:

- Talent development.
- Leadership growth.
- Employee wellbeing.
- Performance excellence.
- Continuous learning.

---

# ARTICLE III

# HUMAN RESOURCES GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

The HR governance structure shall follow:

```
                 BOARD OF TRUSTEES

                         ↓

             EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP

                         ↓

        HUMAN RESOURCES DEPARTMENT

                         ↓

            DEPARTMENT MANAGERS

                         ↓

                 ALL STAFF MEMBERS
```

# ARTICLE IV

# CORE HUMAN RESOURCE PRINCIPLES

## 1. Merit-Based Management

- Competence.
- Experience.
- Performance.
- Integrity.

---

## 2. Equal Opportunity

TJF shall promote fair opportunities based on:

- Ability.
- Qualification.
- Professional conduct.

---

## 3. Professional Excellence

Employees shall be encouraged to pursue:

- Growth.
- Innovation.
- Continuous improvement.

---

## 4. Respect and Inclusion

TJF shall maintain a workplace based on:

- Respect.
- Collaboration.
- Dignity.
- Teamwork.

# ARTICLE XII

# EMPLOYEE ETHICS AND CONDUCT

All TJF employees shall demonstrate:

- Integrity.
- Accountability.
- Respect.
- Confidentiality.
- Professional behaviour.

Employees shall not engage in:

- Fraud.
- Corruption.
- Harassment.
- Discrimination.
- Misuse of institutional resources.
