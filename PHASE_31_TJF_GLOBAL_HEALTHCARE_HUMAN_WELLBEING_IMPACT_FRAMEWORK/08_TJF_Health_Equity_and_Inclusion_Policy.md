# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# HEALTH EQUITY AND INCLUSION POLICY

---

# PREAMBLE

Health is a fundamental human need and an essential foundation for individual and community development.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that healthcare inequalities continue to affect millions of people due to financial limitations, geographic challenges, social barriers, disability, limited infrastructure and unequal access to information.

TJF is committed to creating inclusive healthcare systems that ensure vulnerable and underserved populations receive the support, dignity and opportunities required to achieve better health outcomes.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Policy shall:

- Promote equitable healthcare access.
- Reduce healthcare inequalities.
- Support vulnerable populations.
- Encourage inclusive healthcare systems.
- Address social barriers affecting health.
- Strengthen dignity-centred healthcare delivery.

---

# ARTICLE II

# HEALTH EQUITY VISION

## Vision Statement

> "A world where every person has a fair opportunity to achieve optimal health and wellbeing through inclusive, accessible and compassionate healthcare systems."

---

# ARTICLE III

# CORE PRINCIPLES

TJF health equity initiatives shall be guided by:

## 1. Equality of Opportunity

Every individual should have access to healthcare support based on need.

---

## 2. Inclusion

Healthcare systems must accommodate diverse communities.

---

## 3. Respect and Dignity

Every person must receive respectful and compassionate care.

---

## 4. Accessibility

Healthcare barriers should be identified and reduced.

---

## 5. Community Empowerment

Communities should participate in healthcare solutions.

---

## 6. Social Responsibility

Healthcare improvement requires collective commitment.

- Rural populations.
- Children.
- Older persons.

- Socially vulnerable groups.

- Community health programmes.
- Mobile healthcare outreach.
- Healthcare partnerships.
- Digital health solutions.

# PILLAR 3

TJF shall promote:

- Accessible healthcare facilities.
- Inclusive communication.
