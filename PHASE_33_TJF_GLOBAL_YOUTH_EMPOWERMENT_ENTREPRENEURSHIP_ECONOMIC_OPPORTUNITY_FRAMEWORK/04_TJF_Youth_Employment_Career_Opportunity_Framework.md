# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# YOUTH EMPLOYMENT AND CAREER OPPORTUNITY FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

Employment provides individuals with dignity, economic independence and the opportunity to contribute meaningfully to society.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that many young people face barriers including limited work experience, skills gaps, inadequate career guidance, restricted professional networks and changing labour market demands.

Through this Framework, TJF commits to developing employment pathways that connect young people with skills, opportunities and networks required for successful careers in the modern economy.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Framework shall:

- Improve youth employment opportunities.
- Develop career readiness.
- Strengthen workplace skills.
- Support professional growth.
- Create industry connections.
- Prepare young people for future jobs.

---

# ARTICLE II

# EMPLOYMENT VISION
