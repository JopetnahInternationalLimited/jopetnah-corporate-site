# Government Relations Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes governments as essential partners in advancing sustainable development, improving public services and addressing social challenges.

Governments establish policies, coordinate national development priorities and create enabling environments for organizations working toward community transformation.

This Government Relations Framework establishes TJF’s approach for developing constructive, transparent and strategic relationships with national governments, local authorities, public institutions and policy stakeholders.

The Framework enables TJF to collaborate effectively with governments while maintaining independence, integrity and accountability.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s government engagement approach.
- Strengthen relationships with public institutions.
- Support national development priorities.
- Promote effective public-private collaboration.
- Improve policy engagement.
- Increase institutional credibility.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to engagement with:

- National governments.
- Ministries and government agencies.
- Local authorities.
- Public institutions.
- Development planning bodies.
- Regulatory authorities.
- Public sector partners.

---

# 3. Government Relations Vision

To become a trusted development partner supporting governments in achieving inclusive, sustainable and measurable development outcomes.

---

# 4. Government Relations Mission

To establish professional relationships with public institutions based on cooperation, transparency, shared objectives and respect for national priorities.

---

# 5. Government Engagement Principles

TJF government relations shall follow:

## Respect for Sovereignty

TJF shall respect national laws, policies and development priorities.

---

## Transparency

All government engagements shall be conducted openly and professionally.

---

## Independence

TJF shall maintain institutional integrity and mission focus.

---

## Collaboration

Engagement shall focus on shared solutions.

---

## Accountability

Commitments shall be fulfilled responsibly.

---

## Public Interest

Activities shall prioritize community benefit.

---
