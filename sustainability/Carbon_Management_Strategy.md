# Carbon Management Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that reducing greenhouse gas emissions is essential for addressing climate change and protecting vulnerable communities.

Carbon emissions from energy use, transportation, infrastructure, production systems and consumption patterns contribute significantly to global climate challenges. TJF therefore commits to measuring, reducing and responsibly managing its carbon footprint across institutional operations, programmes and partnerships.

This Carbon Management Strategy establishes TJF’s framework for carbon measurement, emission reduction, sustainable practices and climate accountability.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s carbon management approach.
- Measure institutional greenhouse gas emissions.
- Reduce carbon impacts.
- Promote low-carbon operations.
- Encourage sustainable practices.
- Strengthen climate reporting.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to:

- TJF offices.
- Field operations.
- Development programmes.
- Transportation activities.
- Procurement systems.
- Infrastructure projects.
- Partnerships.

---

# 3. Carbon Management Vision

To become a climate-responsible organization that continuously reduces emissions and promotes sustainable development solutions.

---

# 4. Carbon Management Mission

To measure, reduce and manage carbon emissions through innovation, efficiency, renewable energy adoption and responsible environmental practices.

# 5. Guiding Principles

TJF carbon management shall follow:

## Measurement

Understanding carbon impacts through reliable data.

## Reduction First

Prioritizing emission reductions before offsets.

## Efficiency

Improving resource and energy performance.

---

## Innovation

Using technology and creativity to reduce emissions.

---

## Transparency

Reporting progress responsibly.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Strengthening performance over time.

- Establish carbon measurement systems.

# 7. Carbon Management Framework

TJF carbon management approach:

```
Measure
   ↓
Analyze
   ↓
Reduce
   ↓
Replace
   ↓
Offset Where Necessary
   ↓
Report
   ↓
Improve
```

# 8. Carbon Footprint Assessment

TJF shall assess emissions from:

## Scope 1 Emissions

Direct emissions from sources controlled by TJF.

Examples:

- Organization-owned vehicles.
- Fuel consumption.
- Equipment operations.

---

## Scope 2 Emissions

Indirect emissions from purchased energy.

Examples:

- Electricity consumption.
- Energy supply systems.

---

## Scope 3 Emissions

Other indirect emissions.

Examples:

- Business travel.
- Supply chains.
- Procurement.
- Waste management.
- Partner activities.

# 13. Carbon Offset Approach

Where emissions cannot immediately be eliminated, TJF may consider responsible offset approaches including:

- Reforestation projects.
- Ecosystem restoration.
- Renewable energy projects.
- Community climate initiatives.

Offsets shall complement, not replace, emission reduction efforts.
