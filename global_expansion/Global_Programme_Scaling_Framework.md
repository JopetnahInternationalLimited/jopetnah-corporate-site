# Global Programme Scaling Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) seeks to maximize its global contribution by transforming successful programmes into scalable models capable of reaching communities across countries and regions.

Programme scaling requires more than expansion of activities; it requires a structured approach that preserves effectiveness, adapts to local realities and maintains measurable impact.

This Global Programme Scaling Framework establishes TJF’s methodology for identifying successful initiatives, preparing them for expansion and ensuring sustainable implementation worldwide.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish a global programme scaling methodology.
- Increase the reach of successful initiatives.
- Improve programme replication.
- Maintain quality during expansion.
- Strengthen global impact.

---

# 2. Programme Scaling Vision

To develop TJF programmes into globally recognized models capable of creating sustainable transformation across diverse communities.

---

# 3. Programme Scaling Mission

To identify effective solutions, strengthen implementation models and expand successful programmes through strategic adaptation and collaboration.

---

# 4. Scaling Philosophy

TJF believes:

> "A successful solution creates greater impact when it can be adapted, replicated and sustained beyond its original location."

Effective scaling requires:

- Evidence.
- Adaptability.
- Local ownership.
- Quality control.
- Continuous learning.

# 6. Programme Selection Criteria

Programmes considered for scaling shall demonstrate:

## Proven Impact

Evidence of:

- Positive outcomes.
- Community benefits.
- Measurable results.

---

## Operational Readiness

Including:

- Clear processes.
- Implementation guidelines.
- Required resources.

---

## Adaptability

Ability to function within:

- Different cultures.
- Different countries.
- Different operating environments.

---

## Sustainability

Including:

- Long-term funding.
- Local ownership.
- Institutional support.
