# Green Development Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable development requires an approach that advances economic growth, social inclusion and environmental protection simultaneously.

Traditional development models have often contributed to environmental degradation, resource depletion and climate vulnerability. TJF therefore promotes green development approaches that create economic opportunities while protecting ecosystems and strengthening community resilience.

This Green Development Framework establishes TJF’s commitment to supporting sustainable economies, environmentally responsible investments, green innovation and development models that benefit present and future generations.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Define TJF’s green development approach.
- Promote sustainable economic growth.
- Encourage environmentally responsible practices.
- Support green innovation and entrepreneurship.
- Strengthen climate-resilient communities.
- Integrate environmental considerations into development programmes.
