# Media Relations Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes the media as a critical partner in increasing awareness, promoting transparency, sharing impact stories and strengthening public trust.

A strategic media relations approach enables TJF to communicate its mission effectively, highlight community transformation, engage stakeholders and contribute positively to global development conversations.

This Media Relations Strategy establishes TJF’s framework for building professional relationships with media organizations, journalists and communication platforms while maintaining accuracy, integrity and institutional credibility.

The Strategy provides guidance for media engagement, press communication, interviews, publications and reputation protection.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s media engagement approach.
- Build strong media relationships.
- Increase institutional visibility.
- Promote accurate reporting.
- Strengthen public confidence.
- Support advocacy and awareness efforts.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to:

- Traditional media.
- Digital media.

- Local media organizations.

- Press activities.

# 3. Media Relations Vision

To establish TJF as a trusted and credible source of information on sustainable development, community transformation and social impact.

# 5. Media Relations Principles

TJF media engagement shall follow:

## Transparency

Providing accurate and timely information.

---

## Professionalism

Maintaining respectful relationships with media professionals.

---

## Accuracy

Ensuring all public information is verified.

---

## Accessibility

Supporting responsible access to information.

---

## Respect

Protecting the dignity of communities and stakeholders.

---

## Responsiveness

Providing timely communication when required.

# 12. Interview Management Framework

Before interviews:

- Prepare key messages.
- Review relevant facts.
- Identify objectives.

During interviews:

- Communicate clearly.
- Stay aligned with TJF values.
- Avoid unsupported claims.

After interviews:

- Monitor publication.
- Evaluate impact.
- Maintain relationship.
