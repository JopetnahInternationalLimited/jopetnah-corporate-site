# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# RISK MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

---

# PREAMBLE

The Jopetnah Foundation operates in a complex global environment where institutions face evolving financial, operational, technological, regulatory and strategic challenges.

Effective risk management enables TJF to:

- Protect its mission.
- Safeguard resources.
- Strengthen decision-making.
- Improve resilience.
- Maintain stakeholder confidence.

This Risk Management System establishes the principles and structures required for responsible institutional risk management.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE OF THE RISK MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

## Section 1: Objectives

This System aims to:

- Establish a comprehensive risk management approach.
- Identify potential threats.
- Reduce negative impacts.
- Support strategic decision-making.
- Protect TJF’s institutional legacy.

---

# ARTICLE II

# TJF RISK MANAGEMENT PHILOSOPHY

TJF believes:

> "Effective risk management does not prevent progress; it enables responsible progress."

Risk management shall promote:

- Awareness.
- Prevention.
- Preparedness.
- Accountability.
- Continuous improvement.

---

# ARTICLE III

# RISK GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

The risk governance structure shall follow:

```
                 BOARD OF TRUSTEES

                         ↓

          BOARD RISK AND AUDIT COMMITTEE

                         ↓

             EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP

                         ↓

        RISK MANAGEMENT DEPARTMENT

                         ↓

          DEPARTMENT AND PROGRAMME TEAMS
```

---

# ARTICLE IV

# RISK MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

## 1. Integrated Risk Management

Risk consideration shall be part of all institutional decisions.

---

## 2. Preventive Approach

TJF shall identify risks before they become major challenges.

---

## 3. Accountability

Every department shall be responsible for managing risks within its area.

---

## 4. Continuous Monitoring

Risks shall be regularly reviewed and updated.

---

# TJF ENTERPRISE RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

        ↓

        ↓

Risk Monitoring

        ↓

Risk Reporting

        ↓

# ARTICLE VI

# RISK CATEGORIES

TJF shall monitor the following major risk categories:

---

# 1. Strategic Risks

Risks affecting:

- Mission achievement.
- Institutional growth.
- Strategic direction.

Examples:

- Poor strategic decisions.
- Failure to adapt.
- Loss of institutional relevance.

---

# 2. Financial Risks

Risks affecting:

- Funding.
- Investments.
- Financial sustainability.

Examples:

- Funding shortages.
- Budget weaknesses.
- Financial losses.

---

# 3. Operational Risks

Risks affecting:

- Daily operations.
- Programme delivery.
- Internal systems.

Examples:

- Process failures.
- Resource constraints.
- Service interruptions.

---

# 4. Compliance and Legal Risks

Risks involving:

- Regulatory requirements.
- Legal obligations.
- Policy compliance.

Examples:

- Regulatory violations.
- Contractual disputes.
- Governance failures.

---

# 5. Reputation Risks

Risks affecting:

- Public trust.
- Institutional credibility.
- Stakeholder confidence.

Examples:

- Negative publicity.
- Ethical failures.
- Poor communication.

---

# 6. Technology and Cyber Risks

Risks involving:

- Digital systems.
- Information security.
- Data protection.

Examples:

- Cyber threats.
- System failures.
- Data breaches.

---

# 7. Human Capital Risks

Risks involving:

- Leadership.
- Workforce capacity.
- Staff retention.

Examples:

- Skills shortages.
- Poor succession planning.
- Workplace challenges.

---

# ARTICLE VII

# RISK ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

Risks shall be assessed according to:

## Likelihood

The probability that a risk may occur.

---

## Impact

The potential consequence if the risk occurs.

---

## Risk Rating

A combination of:

```
Likelihood × Impact = Risk Level
```

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# RISK RESPONSE STRATEGIES

TJF may respond through:

## Risk Avoidance

Removing activities creating unacceptable risks.

---

## Risk Reduction

Implementing controls to reduce impact.

---

## Risk Transfer

Sharing risk through:

- Insurance.
- Agreements.
- Partnerships.

---

## Risk Acceptance

Accepting manageable risks with monitoring.

---

# ARTICLE IX

# DEPARTMENTAL RISK MANAGEMENT

Each department shall maintain:

## Risk Register

Containing:

- Identified risks.
- Risk ratings.
- Control measures.
- Responsible persons.

---

## Risk Action Plans

Including:

- Prevention activities.
- Response actions.
- Monitoring schedules.
