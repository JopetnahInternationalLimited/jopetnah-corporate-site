# Regional Hub and Global Office Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) seeks to establish a globally connected institutional presence capable of coordinating programmes, partnerships and operations across multiple regions.

A strategic office and hub structure enables TJF to deliver localized solutions while maintaining global standards, governance discipline and institutional identity.

This Regional Hub and Global Office Framework defines the principles, structures and operating approach for TJF’s international physical presence.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s global office architecture.
- Define regional coordination structures.
- Guide international expansion locations.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Strengthen global collaboration.

---

# 2. Global Office Vision

To establish a worldwide network of TJF offices and regional hubs that enables sustainable impact across continents.

---

# 3. Global Office Mission

To create an interconnected institutional network that supports programme delivery, partnerships, innovation and regional transformation.

---

# 4. Office Network Philosophy

TJF believes:

> "A global institution requires both a global vision and a local presence."

The office network shall balance:

- Global coordination.
- Regional expertise.
- Local ownership.
- Operational efficiency.

# 10. Office Establishment Criteria

Before establishing a TJF office, assessments shall consider:

## Strategic Need

Questions:

- Does the location support TJF’s mission?
- Is there significant impact opportunity?

---

## Partnership Opportunity

Assessment:

- Government relationships.
- Institutional partners.
- Community networks.

---

## Operational Feasibility

Assessment:

- Legal environment.
- Financial sustainability.
- Human resources availability.

---

## Long-Term Sustainability

Assessment:

- Growth potential.
- Programme continuity.
