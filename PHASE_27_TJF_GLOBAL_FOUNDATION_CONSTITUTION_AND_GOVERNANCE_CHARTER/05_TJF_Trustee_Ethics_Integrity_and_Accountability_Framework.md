# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# TRUSTEE ETHICS, INTEGRITY AND ACCOUNTABILITY FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

The position of Trustee within The Jopetnah Foundation represents a position of trust, responsibility and service.

Trustees are entrusted with safeguarding:

- TJF’s mission.
- Institutional reputation.
- Financial resources.
- Community confidence.
- Future legacy.

This Framework establishes the ethical standards required from all Trustees and governance leaders.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE OF THE FRAMEWORK

## Section 1: Objective

This Framework aims to:

- Define Trustee ethical responsibilities.
- Promote responsible governance.
- Prevent conflicts of interest.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Protect institutional integrity.

---

## Section 2: Ethical Foundation

All Trustee actions shall be guided by:

- Integrity.
- Honesty.
- Fairness.
- Independence.
- Transparency.
- Service.

---

# ARTICLE II

# TRUSTEE CODE OF CONDUCT

Every Trustee shall commit to:

## 1. Act in TJF’s Best Interest

Trustees shall place the interests of TJF above:

- Personal benefit.
- Private interests.
- External pressures.

---

## 2. Exercise Responsible Judgment

Trustees shall:

- Seek adequate information.
- Consider long-term consequences.
- Make informed decisions.

---

## 3. Protect Confidential Information

Trustees shall protect:

- Strategic information.
- Financial information.
- Beneficiary information.
- Internal discussions.

---

## 4. Maintain Professional Conduct

Trustees shall demonstrate:

- Respect.
- Professionalism.
- Responsible communication.

---

# ARTICLE III

# TRUSTEE INTEGRITY PRINCIPLES

## Principle 1: Honesty

Trustees shall communicate truthfully and accurately.

---

## Principle 2: Accountability

Trustees shall accept responsibility for decisions made under their authority.

---

## Principle 3: Stewardship

Trustees shall protect TJF resources as entrusted assets.

---

## Principle 4: Impartiality

Trustees shall make decisions based on institutional objectives rather than personal preferences.

---

# ARTICLE IV

# CONFLICT OF INTEREST POLICY

## Section 1: Definition

A conflict of interest occurs when personal interests could influence or appear to influence Trustee decisions.

---

## Section 2: Examples of Conflicts

Potential conflicts include:

- Personal financial interests.
- Family interests.
- Business relationships.
- External commitments.
- Preferential treatment.

---

## Section 3: Conflict Disclosure

Trustees shall:

- Declare potential conflicts.
- Update disclosures regularly.
- Avoid participating in conflicted decisions.

---

# ARTICLE V

# TRUSTEE INDEPENDENCE

Trustees shall maintain independence by:

- Avoiding undue influence.
- Exercising objective judgment.
- Challenging decisions constructively.
- Protecting institutional interests.

---

# ARTICLE VI

# FINANCIAL ETHICS

Trustees shall ensure:

- Responsible use of resources.
- Proper financial oversight.
- Accurate reporting.
- Prevention of misuse.

Trustees shall never:

- Personally benefit improperly from TJF resources.
- Authorize unethical transactions.
- Ignore financial concerns.

---

# ARTICLE VII

# TRANSPARENCY REQUIREMENTS

TJF shall promote transparency through:

- Governance reporting.
- Financial accountability.
- Ethical disclosures.
- Institutional reviews.

Trustees shall support open and responsible governance practices.

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# TRUSTEE ACCOUNTABILITY SYSTEM

Trustee accountability shall include:

```
Ethical Standards

        ↓

Performance Expectations

        ↓

Governance Review

        ↓

Corrective Action

        ↓

Continuous Improvement
```

---

# ARTICLE IX

# TRUSTEE PERFORMANCE EXPECTATIONS

Trustees shall be evaluated based on:

## Governance Contribution

- Participation.
- Strategic input.
- Oversight effectiveness.

---

## Ethical Conduct

- Integrity.
- Compliance.
- Professional behaviour.

---

## Institutional Contribution

- Support for TJF growth.
- Protection of mission.
- Advancement of legacy.

---

# ARTICLE X

# REPORTING ETHICAL CONCERNS

TJF shall maintain mechanisms for reporting:

- Misconduct.
- Governance concerns.
- Ethical violations.
- Financial irregularities.

Reports shall be handled with:

- Confidentiality.
- Fairness.
- Due process.

---

# ARTICLE XI

# VIOLATIONS AND CORRECTIVE ACTION

Where ethical violations occur, TJF may apply:

- Counselling.
- Formal warning.
- Governance review.
- Suspension.
- Removal from Trustee position.

All actions shall follow due process.

---

# ARTICLE XII

# ANTI-CORRUPTION COMMITMENT

TJF maintains zero tolerance toward:

- Corruption.
- Bribery.
- Fraud.
- Abuse of authority.
- Misuse of institutional resources.

---

# ARTICLE XIII

# TRUSTEE RESPONSIBILITY TO BENEFICIARIES

Trustees shall ensure that TJF activities respect:

- Human dignity.
- Community needs.
- Fair access.
- Social responsibility.

---

# ARTICLE XIV

# ETHICAL LEADERSHIP LEGACY

Trustees shall be remembered not only by decisions made, but by:

- Values protected.
- People empowered.
- Systems strengthened.
- Legacy preserved.

---

# FINAL TRUSTEE ETHICS DECLARATION

> "A Trustee of The Jopetnah Foundation accepts a responsibility greater than authority: the responsibility to protect a mission entrusted to generations."
