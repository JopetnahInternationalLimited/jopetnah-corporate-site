Code of Ethics & Business Conduct

This Code sets out the ethical standards and professional conduct expected of everyone associated with Jopetnah International Limited.

Purpose

Our Ethical Standard

Jopetnah International Limited’s Code of Ethics & Business Conduct establishes the standards of integrity, professionalism, fairness, respect and accountability expected across the Company. It supports responsible delivery of our services and protects the trust placed in us by clients, employees, suppliers, partners, institutions and communities.

The Code applies to directors, employees, consultants, contractors and other persons acting for or representing the Company, across our 16 strategic sectors and international business relationships.

Core Values

Principles That Guide Our Conduct

Integrity

Act honestly, transparently and lawfully, even where no one is watching.

Excellence

Pursue competent, reliable, professional and fit-for-purpose delivery.

Accountability

Take responsibility for decisions, commitments, records and outcomes.

Collaboration

Work constructively with colleagues, clients, suppliers and partners.

Safety & Sustainability

Protect people, resources and the environment while creating lasting value.

Customer-Centricity

Understand legitimate client needs and deliver practical, responsible solutions.

Innovation

Seek better approaches while managing quality, security, risk and unintended consequences.

Respect

Protect dignity, equal opportunity and fair treatment in our workplaces and relationships.

Conduct

Standards We Expect

1. Compliance with Law

Comply with applicable laws, regulations, licences, contractual obligations and Company policies.

2. Conflicts of Interest

Disclose actual, potential or perceived conflicts promptly and do not allow personal interests to improperly influence Company decisions.

3. Fair Dealing

Deal honestly with clients, competitors, suppliers, employees and partners. Do not misrepresent capabilities, prices, commitments or results.

4. Anti-Corruption

Do not offer, request or accept bribes, kickbacks, facilitation payments or other improper advantages.

5. Respectful Workplace

Maintain a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, forced labour, child labour and abuse.

6. Health, Safety & Environment

Follow applicable HSE requirements and act responsibly toward people, communities and the environment.

7. Confidentiality & Data Protection

Protect confidential information, personal data, intellectual property and commercially sensitive information.

8. Company Assets

Use funds, equipment, systems, vehicles, documents and intellectual property responsibly and for legitimate purposes.

9. Accurate Records

Maintain complete, accurate and timely financial, operational and project records.

10. Responsible Communications

Only authorised persons speak on behalf of the Company. Do not knowingly publish confidential or misleading information.

11. Professional Boundaries

Do not misuse Company authority, relationships or information for personal gain.

People

Human Rights, Fairness &Inclusion

Jopetnah seeks a professional environment in which people are treated with dignity and fairness. Employment and engagement decisions should be based on legitimate criteria such as qualifications, performance, conduct, business needs and applicable law. The Company does not tolerate forced labour or child labour and expects suppliers and partners to respect applicable labour standards.

Responsible Business

Clients, Partners & Communities

Our commercial activities should be conducted responsibly and with awareness of their wider effects. We seek to build durable relationships, protect stakeholder interests, respect communities and consider environmental and social risks in proportion to the activity.

The Code complements the Company’s sustainability commitments and the social-impact work of The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF), while remaining distinct from TJF’s nonprofit mandate.

Speak Up

Raising Concerns

Anyone who becomes aware of suspected misconduct, a serious breach of this Code or unlawful conduct is encouraged to report it through the Whistleblowing / Speak-Up Policy. Good-faith reporting is protected from retaliation.

Questions about ethical conduct may be directed through appropriate management, Human Resources, the Company Secretary or other authorised channels.

Governance

Responsibility & Review

The Managing Director and senior leadership are responsible for promoting ethical conduct, while every person covered by the Code is responsible for complying with it. The Code will be reviewed periodically and updated when necessary.
