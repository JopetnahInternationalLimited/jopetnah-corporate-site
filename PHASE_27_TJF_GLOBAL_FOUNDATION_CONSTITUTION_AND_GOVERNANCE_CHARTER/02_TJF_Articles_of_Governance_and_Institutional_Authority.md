# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# ARTICLES OF GOVERNANCE AND INSTITUTIONAL AUTHORITY

---

# PREAMBLE

These Articles of Governance establish the authority structure, governance relationships and institutional control mechanisms of The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF).

The purpose of this document is to ensure that TJF operates through:

- Responsible authority.
- Transparent governance.
- Strategic leadership.
- Ethical accountability.
- Institutional continuity.

---

# ARTICLE I

# GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

## Section 1: Governance Foundation

TJF governance shall be based on:

- Integrity.
- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Independence.
- Stewardship.
- Strategic responsibility.

---

## Section 2: Separation of Authority

TJF shall maintain clear separation between:

### Governance Authority

Responsible for:

- Direction.
- Oversight.
- Protection of mission.

---

### Executive Authority

Responsible for:

- Implementation.
- Operations.
- Institutional performance.

---

### Operational Authority

Responsible for:

- Programme delivery.
- Daily execution.
- Service management.

---

# ARTICLE II

# INSTITUTIONAL GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

The governance structure of TJF shall be:

```
                TJF CONSTITUTION

                       ↓

             BOARD OF TRUSTEES

                       ↓

          GOVERNANCE COMMITTEES

                       ↓

          EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP

                       ↓

        MANAGEMENT AND OPERATIONS

                       ↓

          PROGRAMMES AND IMPACT
```

---

# ARTICLE III

# BOARD OF TRUSTEES AUTHORITY

## Section 1: Supreme Governance Authority

The Board of Trustees shall represent the highest governance authority of TJF.

The Board shall protect:

- Institutional mission.
- Constitutional principles.
- Long-term sustainability.
- Public trust.

---

## Section 2: Board Powers

The Board shall have authority to:

- Approve institutional strategy.
- Establish governance policies.
- Appoint senior leadership.
- Approve major investments.
- Protect institutional assets.
- Review organisational performance.
- Ensure ethical compliance.

---

# ARTICLE IV

# EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP AUTHORITY

## Section 1: Executive Responsibility

Executive leadership shall be responsible for implementing the strategic direction approved by the Board.

---

## Section 2: Executive Powers

Executive leadership shall:

- Manage institutional operations.
- Develop programmes.
- Lead staff.
- Execute approved strategies.
- Manage partnerships.
- Report performance.

---

## Section 3: Executive Limitations

Executive leadership shall not:

- Alter TJF’s constitutional purpose.
- Override Board authority.
- Compromise institutional values.
- Misuse institutional resources.

---

# ARTICLE V

# GOVERNANCE COMMITTEE SYSTEM

TJF shall establish specialised committees to strengthen governance.

---

# 1. Governance and Ethics Committee

Responsibilities:

- Review governance standards.
- Monitor ethical compliance.
- Recommend governance improvements.

---

# 2. Finance and Investment Committee

Responsibilities:

- Review financial strategy.
- Monitor investments.
- Protect institutional resources.

---

# 3. Audit and Risk Committee

Responsibilities:

- Review controls.
- Monitor institutional risks.
- Strengthen accountability.

---

# 4. Programme and Impact Committee

Responsibilities:

- Evaluate programmes.
- Monitor social impact.
- Review effectiveness.

---

# 5. Leadership and Succession Committee

Responsibilities:

- Support leadership continuity.
- Review executive development.
- Protect institutional stability.

---

# ARTICLE VI

# DECISION-MAKING AUTHORITY FRAMEWORK

TJF decisions shall follow:

```
Strategic Decisions

        ↓

Board Approval

        ↓

Executive Implementation

        ↓

Operational Execution

        ↓

Performance Review
```

---

# ARTICLE VII

# RESERVED POWERS OF THE BOARD

Certain decisions shall require Board approval.

These include:

- Constitutional amendments.
- Major asset decisions.
- Institutional restructuring.
- Appointment of senior executives.
- Major partnerships.
- Endowment decisions.
- Strategic direction changes.

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# MANAGEMENT AUTHORITY FRAMEWORK

Management teams shall have authority to:

- Execute approved plans.
- Manage assigned resources.
- Coordinate operations.
- Deliver programmes.
- Monitor results.

Management authority shall always remain within approved policies and budgets.

---

# ARTICLE IX

# ACCOUNTABILITY FRAMEWORK

Every level of TJF shall maintain accountability.

## Board Accountability

Responsible for:

- Strategic oversight.
- Governance integrity.
- Institutional protection.

---

## Executive Accountability

Responsible for:

- Performance delivery.
- Operational excellence.
- Resource management.

---

## Staff Accountability

Responsible for:

- Professional conduct.
- Effective execution.
- Alignment with TJF values.

---

# ARTICLE X

# GOVERNANCE TRANSPARENCY

TJF shall promote transparency through:

- Annual reports.
- Financial reporting.
- Impact reporting.
- Governance reviews.
- Internal evaluations.

---

# ARTICLE XI

# CONFLICT OF AUTHORITY MANAGEMENT

Where governance disagreements arise:

The resolution process shall be:

```
Identify Issue

      ↓

Review Constitutional Principles

      ↓

Consult Relevant Authority

      ↓

Reach Institutional Decision

      ↓

Document Outcome
```

---

# ARTICLE XII

# INSTITUTIONAL PROTECTION AUTHORITY

The Board shall have responsibility to protect TJF from:

- Mission deviation.
- Financial misuse.
- Governance failures.
- Reputation risks.
- Institutional instability.

---

# ARTICLE XIII

# FUTURE GOVERNANCE EVOLUTION

TJF governance systems may evolve to respond to:

- Institutional growth.
- Global expansion.
- Technological change.
- Emerging challenges.

However, all evolution shall preserve:

- Founding purpose.
- Core values.
- Constitutional principles.

---

# FINAL GOVERNANCE DECLARATION

> "Authority within The Jopetnah Foundation exists not for personal control, but for responsible stewardship of a mission entrusted to generations."
