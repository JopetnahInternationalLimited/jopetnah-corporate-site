# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# CLEAN WATER ACCESS AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

Clean water is a fundamental requirement for healthy communities, sustainable development and human wellbeing.

Millions of people globally continue to face challenges caused by inadequate access to safe water, including preventable diseases, reduced educational opportunities, limited economic activity and increased vulnerability.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that sustainable water access requires more than infrastructure development. It requires community participation, education, maintenance systems and long-term partnerships.

Through this Programme, TJF commits to supporting communities in achieving reliable access to safe and sustainable water resources.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Expand access to clean water.
- Support community water systems.
- Improve public health outcomes.
- Strengthen local water management.
- Promote water accessibility and inclusion.
- Develop sustainable community water solutions.
