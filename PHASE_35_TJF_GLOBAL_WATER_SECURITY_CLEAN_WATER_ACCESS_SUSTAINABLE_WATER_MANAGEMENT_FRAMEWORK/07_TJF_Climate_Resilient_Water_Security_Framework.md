# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# CLIMATE RESILIENT WATER SECURITY FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

Climate change is increasing pressure on global water systems through droughts, floods, unpredictable rainfall patterns, rising temperatures and ecosystem disruption.

Communities that depend on vulnerable water sources face increasing risks to health, agriculture, livelihoods and economic stability.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that climate-resilient water systems are essential for protecting communities and ensuring sustainable development.

Through this Framework, TJF commits to supporting adaptive water solutions that strengthen resilience, reduce vulnerability and prepare communities for future climate challenges.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Framework shall:

- Strengthen climate-resilient water systems.
- Reduce water-related climate risks.
- Support drought and flood preparedness.
- Promote adaptive water infrastructure.
- Encourage climate-smart water management.
- Protect vulnerable communities.
