# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# ENVIRONMENTAL RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

Environmental challenges are increasingly affecting communities, institutions and development initiatives worldwide.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that effective environmental risk management is essential for protecting people, preserving resources and maintaining operational continuity.

This Framework establishes a proactive approach to identifying environmental risks, reducing vulnerabilities and strengthening resilience.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Framework shall:

- Establish environmental risk governance.
- Identify environmental threats.
- Strengthen institutional resilience.
- Support responsible decision-making.
- Reduce environmental harm.
- Improve preparedness and response capabilities.

---

# ARTICLE II

# ENVIRONMENTAL RISK VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To build an environmentally resilient institution capable of anticipating, managing and reducing risks while protecting communities and ecosystems."

---

# ARTICLE III

# GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Environmental risk management at TJF shall follow:

## 1. Prevention

Reducing risks before environmental harm occurs.

---

## 2. Preparedness

Building capacity to respond effectively.

---

## 3. Evidence-Based Decisions

Using reliable information and analysis.

---

## 4. Resilience

Strengthening systems against future challenges.

---

## 5. Accountability

Assigning clear responsibility for environmental risk management.

---

## 6. Continuous Improvement

Learning from experience and emerging risks.

# ARTICLE V

# ENVIRONMENTAL RISK CATEGORIES

TJF shall monitor and manage risks including:

---

# 1. Climate Risks

Including:

- Extreme weather events.
- Flooding.
- Drought.
- Rising temperatures.
- Climate-related disruptions.

---

# 2. Biodiversity Risks

Including:

- Habitat degradation.
- Ecosystem loss.
- Unsustainable resource use.
- Environmental imbalance.

---

# 3. Water Risks

Including:

- Water scarcity.
- Pollution.
- Reduced access to clean water.
- Watershed degradation.

---

# 4. Pollution Risks

Including:

- Waste contamination.
- Air pollution.
- Chemical exposure.
- Improper disposal practices.

---

# 5. Operational Environmental Risks

Including:

- Unsustainable facilities.
- Inefficient resource use.
- Infrastructure vulnerabilities.
- Supply chain impacts.
