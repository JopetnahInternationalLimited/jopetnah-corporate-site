## About Us

Jopetnah International Limited is a diversified international conglomerate and social enterprise committed to delivering innovative, integrated, and sustainable solutions across sixteen strategic sectors.

Established in 2016 and headquartered in Kampala, Uganda, the company was founded on the belief that today's complex challenges require multidisciplinary expertise, strategic partnerships, and a relentless commitment to excellence.

Our integrated business model enables governments, private enterprises, development partners, investors, educational institutions, NGOs, and communities to access a comprehensive portfolio of professional services through one trusted corporate partner.

### Our Story

What began as a consultancy-driven enterprise has evolved into a diversified international organization serving clients across multiple industries and geographical markets. Today, our multidisciplinary capabilities span strategic advisory, engineering, technology, healthcare, logistics, agriculture, energy, education, tourism, international recruitment, marketing, mining, events management, research and development, FMCG, and international trade.

### The Jopetnah Difference

Our greatest strength lies not in operating across multiple industries — but in integrating them. Our integrated approach allows us to:

- Deliver end-to-end solutions from concept to completion
- Combine expertise across multiple industries to solve complex challenges
- Improve operational efficiency through coordinated service delivery
- Foster innovation by connecting diverse disciplines
- Build long-term partnerships founded on trust, integrity, and measurable impact
- Create sustainable value for businesses, institutions, and communities

## Technology Stack

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework, no build tools, no dependencies.

- **HTML5 / CSS3 / vanilla JavaScript (ES6)**
- **Fonts:** Poppins (headings/nav) and Lora (body text), via Google Fonts CDN
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages, auto-deployed from the `main` branch
- **Metadata:** Open Graph, Twitter Cards, and Schema.org `Organization` JSON-LD on every page
- **Automated QA:** GitHub Actions workflow runs HTML validation and broken-link checks on every push

See [`docs/development-guide.md`](docs/development-guide.md) for the full architecture rationale, including why this project deliberately avoids a build pipeline at its current size.
