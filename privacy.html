Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Jopetnah International Limited (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

When you use our Contact page, we collect the information you voluntarily submit, which may include your name, email address, organisation, and the content of your message.

This information is submitted through a form processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. We do not collect personal information through automated tracking unless you explicitly submit it via our contact form.

Information submitted through our Contact page is used solely to respond to your enquiry, discuss potential collaboration, or provide the services you have requested. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Our contact form is powered by Formspree. When you submit the form, the data is sent to Formspree’s servers and is subject to Formspree’s own privacy policy in addition to this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review Formspree’s privacy practices.

This website does not currently use cookies or third-party analytics tracking. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly, and the “Last updated” date above will reflect that change.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain contact form submissions only for as long as necessary to respond to and resolve your enquiry. We take reasonable measures to protect any information you share with us from unauthorised access or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have submitted to us by contacting us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests in accordance with applicable law.
