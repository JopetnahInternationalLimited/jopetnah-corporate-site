# TJF Research Institute Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable development requires strong research institutions capable of generating knowledge, influencing policies, developing innovations and supporting evidence-based decision-making.

The establishment of the **Jopetnah Foundation Research Institute (JFRI)** will position TJF as a global knowledge institution dedicated to advancing research, innovation and practical solutions for social, economic and environmental challenges.

JFRI will serve as a multidisciplinary research centre connecting academics, policymakers, communities, innovators and development partners to generate impactful knowledge and scalable solutions.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish the structure of the TJF Research Institute.
- Define its mandate and strategic direction.
- Create research governance systems.
- Promote global research partnerships.
- Strengthen evidence-based development.
- Support policy influence and innovation.
