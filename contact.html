Our Presence

Rooted in Uganda. Open to the Right Opportunities.

Jopetnah International Limited is headquartered in Kampala, Uganda. We maintain a network of professional contacts and collaborators across several regions of Uganda and undertake selective cross-border engagements through our wider international business network.
