# Digital Learning Platform Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes digital learning as a powerful tool for expanding access to knowledge, strengthening skills development and supporting lifelong learning.

Technology has transformed how individuals, organizations and communities access education and professional development. TJF therefore seeks to establish a digital learning ecosystem that delivers high-quality, accessible and practical learning opportunities globally.

This Digital Learning Platform Framework establishes TJF’s strategic approach for developing online learning systems, digital training programmes, professional development pathways and knowledge-sharing platforms.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s digital learning ecosystem.
- Expand access to education and training.
- Promote lifelong learning.
- Support digital skills development.
- Deliver scalable knowledge solutions.
- Strengthen global learning communities.

---

- Online courses.
