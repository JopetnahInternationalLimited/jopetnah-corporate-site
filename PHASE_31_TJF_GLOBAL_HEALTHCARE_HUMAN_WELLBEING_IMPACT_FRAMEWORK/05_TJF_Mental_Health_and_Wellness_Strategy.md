# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLNESS STRATEGY

---

# PREAMBLE

Mental health is a fundamental part of human wellbeing, affecting how individuals think, feel, interact, work and contribute to society.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that communities worldwide continue to experience mental health challenges arising from social pressures, economic difficulties, trauma, displacement, health conditions and changing global environments.

TJF commits to advancing mental wellness through awareness, prevention, support services, partnerships and innovative solutions that promote healthier individuals and communities.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Strategy shall:

- Promote mental health awareness.
- Reduce stigma associated with mental health challenges.
- Expand access to psychological support.
- Strengthen community wellness systems.
- Support vulnerable groups.
- Promote emotional resilience.

---

# ARTICLE II

# MENTAL HEALTH VISION

## Vision Statement

> "A world where every individual has the knowledge, support and opportunity to achieve mental wellbeing, emotional resilience and a healthy quality of life."

---

# ARTICLE III

# GUIDING PRINCIPLES

TJF mental health initiatives shall follow:

## 1. Human Dignity

Every person deserves respect, compassion and support.

---

## 2. Inclusion

Mental healthcare should be accessible to all communities.

---

## 3. Prevention

Early awareness and intervention improve outcomes.

---

## 4. Confidentiality

Personal wellbeing information must be protected.

---

## 5. Compassion

Support systems must be people-centred.

---

## 6. Evidence-Based Practice

Programmes shall be guided by reliable knowledge and best practices.
