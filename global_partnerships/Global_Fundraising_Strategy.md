# Global Fundraising Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable development requires reliable, diversified and strategic financial resources.

Global fundraising is not only the process of securing financial contributions; it is the development of meaningful relationships with individuals, institutions and communities that share TJF’s commitment to creating lasting social impact.

This Global Fundraising Strategy establishes TJF’s approach for mobilizing financial resources through philanthropy, grants, partnerships, digital platforms, campaigns, corporate engagement and innovative financing mechanisms.

The Strategy provides a structured pathway for expanding TJF’s financial capacity while maintaining transparency, accountability and donor confidence.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s global fundraising framework.
- Diversify funding sources.
- Strengthen fundraising systems.
- Increase donor participation.
- Develop sustainable funding pipelines.
- Support long-term programme implementation.
