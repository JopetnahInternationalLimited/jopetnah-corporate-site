# Corporate Partnership Model

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that the private sector plays a critical role in advancing sustainable development through innovation, investment, technology, employment creation and responsible business practices.

Corporations possess significant resources, expertise and networks that can contribute to solving complex social and economic challenges.

This Corporate Partnership Model establishes TJF’s framework for developing strategic relationships with businesses, corporations and industry leaders through Corporate Social Investment (CSI), shared-value partnerships, sponsorships, innovation collaborations and strategic alliances.

The Model moves beyond traditional corporate donations by creating long-term partnerships where businesses and communities jointly achieve measurable impact.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Model aims to:

- Establish TJF’s corporate engagement framework.
- Develop sustainable private-sector partnerships.
- Increase corporate participation in social impact initiatives.
- Mobilize corporate resources and expertise.
- Create shared-value opportunities.
- Strengthen institutional sustainability.

---

This Model applies to partnerships with:

- Multinational corporations.
- Local businesses.
- Industry associations.
- Financial institutions.
- Technology companies.
- Manufacturing companies.
- Energy companies.
- Professional service providers.
- Social enterprises.

# 3. Corporate Partnership Vision

To become a trusted partner for businesses seeking to create measurable social, economic and environmental impact.

# 4. Corporate Partnership Mission

To build innovative partnerships that combine corporate capabilities with TJF’s development expertise to create sustainable solutions.

# 5. Corporate Partnership Principles

TJF corporate partnerships shall be guided by:

## Shared Value Creation

Partnerships must benefit communities, corporations and TJF.

---

## Strategic Alignment

Corporate collaborations must support TJF’s mission.

---

## Integrity

All partnerships must maintain ethical standards.

---

## Transparency

Partnership objectives and responsibilities must be clear.

---

## Sustainability

Partnerships should create long-term value.

---

## Impact Measurement

Results must be measurable and documented.
