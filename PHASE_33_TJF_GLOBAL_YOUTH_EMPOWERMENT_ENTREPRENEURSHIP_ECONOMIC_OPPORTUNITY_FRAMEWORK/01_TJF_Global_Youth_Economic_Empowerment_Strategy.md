# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# GLOBAL YOUTH ECONOMIC EMPOWERMENT STRATEGY

---

# PREAMBLE

Young people represent one of the greatest sources of creativity, innovation and economic potential in the world.

However, many young people continue to face barriers including unemployment, limited access to capital, inadequate business skills, insufficient mentorship and restricted economic opportunities.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that sustainable youth empowerment requires more than temporary support. It requires the creation of systems that enable young people to develop skills, create enterprises, access markets and participate meaningfully in economic growth.

Through this Strategy, TJF commits to building pathways that transform young people from economic dependents into innovators, entrepreneurs, professionals and job creators.

---

This Strategy shall:

- Establish TJF's youth economic empowerment direction.
- Promote entrepreneurship and innovation.
- Support youth employment opportunities.

- Build sustainable economic ecosystems.
