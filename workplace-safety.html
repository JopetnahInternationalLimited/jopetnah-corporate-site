This page sets out JIL’s supporting policy or guideline. Implementation is subject to applicable law, assigned responsibilities and relevant project-specific requirements.

Policy Statement

Our Commitment

Safety is a shared responsibility and must be integrated into planning, execution and review.

Guiding principle: No operational objective takes priority over taking reasonable steps to protect people from serious and preventable harm.

People & Workplace

Purpose

JIL is committed to protecting the health, safety and well-being of employees, contractors, visitors and other people who may be affected by its activities. These guidelines establish basic expectations for identifying hazards, preventing harm and responding to workplace safety concerns.

People & Workplace

Scope

These guidelines apply to JIL offices, training venues, events, project sites and other locations where the company exercises control or has relevant responsibilities. Additional site-specific procedures may be required for construction, transport, engineering, energy, industrial or other higher-risk activities.

People & Workplace

Core Requirements

  • Provide and maintain safe working arrangements in accordance with applicable requirements.
  • Identify hazards and assess relevant workplace and project risks before and during activities.
  • Apply appropriate controls to eliminate hazards where possible or reduce risks to an acceptable level.
  • Provide suitable personal protective equipment where required and ensure personnel understand its proper use.
  • Provide relevant safety information, instruction and training.
  • Maintain appropriate emergency arrangements, including evacuation procedures and access to emergency assistance.
  • Keep work areas orderly and equipment in safe working condition.
  • Encourage prompt reporting of hazards, incidents and near-misses.
  • Investigate significant incidents and implement corrective measures.
  • Require contractors and partners to comply with relevant safety requirements applicable to their work.
People & Workplace

Project-Specific Safety

Before undertaking activities with significant safety risks, the responsible project lead should ensure that appropriate risk assessments, competent personnel, permits, equipment, supervision and emergency arrangements are in place. Where specialist technical controls are required, qualified personnel should be engaged.

People & Workplace

Incident Reporting

All personnel should promptly report injuries, unsafe conditions, near-misses and other safety concerns through the designated reporting channel. Immediate danger should be addressed without delay, including stopping unsafe work where necessary and arranging emergency assistance.

People & Workplace

Responsibilities

Management provides oversight and appropriate resources. Supervisors and project leads communicate safety requirements and monitor compliance. Every individual is responsible for following applicable safety instructions and reporting hazards.

People & Workplace

Review

Safety arrangements should be reviewed after significant incidents, material changes in work activities or identification of new risks. These guidelines will be reviewed at least annually.

Governance

Accountability & Related Policies

Management is responsible for ensuring that this document is communicated to relevant personnel and reviewed when necessary. Implementation should be proportionate to the nature and scale of JIL’s activities and consistent with applicable legal, contractual and site-specific requirements.

Read this document alongside the company’s relevant governance, risk management, procurement, health and safety, ethics, privacy and sustainability materials, as applicable.
