# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# DATA GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes data as a strategic institutional asset that supports informed decision-making, operational excellence, innovation and sustainable development.

This Framework establishes governance structures, responsibilities and standards for managing data throughout its lifecycle while ensuring integrity, privacy, security and responsible use.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

## Objectives

This Framework shall:

- Establish enterprise data governance.
- Improve data quality.
- Protect sensitive information.
- Support evidence-based decision-making.
- Enable responsible data sharing.
- Strengthen regulatory compliance.

---

# ARTICLE II

# DATA GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

TJF shall manage data according to the following principles:

## Accuracy

Data shall be complete, reliable and validated.

---

## Consistency

The same data shall maintain the same meaning across all systems.

---

## Security

Data shall be protected against unauthorized access, alteration and loss.

---

## Privacy

Personal and confidential information shall be handled lawfully and ethically.

---

## Transparency

Data management practices shall be documented and understood.

---

## Accountability

Every data asset shall have a clearly assigned owner and steward.

---

# ARTICLE IV

# DATA ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Data Owners

Responsible for:

- Business accountability.
- Data quality oversight.
- Access approval.
- Compliance.

---

## Data Stewards

Responsible for:

- Day-to-day management.
- Data standards.
- Metadata maintenance.
- Data quality monitoring.

---

## Data Custodians

Responsible for:

- Technical storage.
- Infrastructure.
- Backup.
- Security controls.

---

## Data Users

Responsible for:

- Ethical use.
- Accuracy.
- Compliance with policies.
- Reporting data issues.

---

# ARTICLE V

# DATA CLASSIFICATION

Institutional data shall be classified as:

## Public Data

Approved for public dissemination.

---

## Internal Data

Operational information for authorized staff.

---

## Confidential Data

Sensitive business information requiring controlled access.

---

## Restricted Data

Highly sensitive information requiring enhanced security controls.

Examples include:

- Beneficiary personal information.
- Financial credentials.
- Authentication secrets.
- Legal investigations.

---

# ARTICLE VII

# DATA QUALITY MANAGEMENT

The Foundation shall maintain data quality through:

- Validation rules.
- Standardized formats.
- Duplicate prevention.
- Periodic cleansing.
- Quality audits.

Key quality dimensions include:

- Accuracy.
- Completeness.
- Timeliness.
- Consistency.
- Uniqueness.
- Validity.

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# MASTER DATA MANAGEMENT

TJF shall establish master records for critical entities, including:

- Beneficiaries.
- Donors.
- Partners.
- Employees.
- Suppliers.
- Projects.
- Programmes.
- Geographic locations.

Master data shall be maintained centrally to ensure consistency across all systems.

---

# ARTICLE IX

# METADATA MANAGEMENT

All enterprise datasets shall include standardized metadata describing:

- Source.
- Owner.
- Purpose.
- Classification.
- Update frequency.
- Retention requirements.

---

# ARTICLE X

# DATA ACCESS MANAGEMENT

Access to institutional data shall follow:

- Least privilege principle.
- Role-based access control.
- Multi-factor authentication where appropriate.
- Regular access reviews.

Access rights shall be revoked promptly when no longer required.

---

# ARTICLE XI

# DATA PRIVACY

TJF shall protect personal information by:

- Collecting only necessary data.
- Obtaining appropriate consent where required.
- Limiting disclosure.
- Protecting confidentiality.
- Respecting applicable privacy laws.

Special protection shall be applied to data relating to children and other vulnerable groups.

---

# ARTICLE XII

# DATA SHARING

Data sharing shall occur only where:

- There is a legitimate institutional purpose.
- Appropriate authorization has been obtained.
- Confidentiality obligations are maintained.
- Legal and contractual requirements are satisfied.

Formal data-sharing agreements shall be used where appropriate.

---

# ARTICLE XIII

# DATA ANALYTICS

Enterprise analytics shall support:

- Programme evaluation.
- Strategic planning.
- Financial management.
- Risk management.
- Monitoring and evaluation.
- Institutional performance.

Analytics shall be conducted using verified and high-quality data.

---

# ARTICLE XIV

# DATA SECURITY

The Foundation shall protect enterprise data through:

- Encryption.
- Secure backups.
- Network protection.
- Endpoint security.
- Security monitoring.
- Disaster recovery.

Security controls shall be reviewed regularly.

---

# ARTICLE XV

# DATA COMPLIANCE

Data governance activities shall comply with:

- Applicable national laws.
- Donor requirements.
- Contractual obligations.
- TJF internal policies.
- Ethical standards.

Periodic compliance assessments shall be conducted.

---

# ARTICLE XVI

# DATA GOVERNANCE PERFORMANCE INDICATORS

Success shall be measured through:

- Data quality scores.
- Number of governance violations.
- Compliance rates.
- Timeliness of reporting.
- User satisfaction.
- Data availability.

---

# ARTICLE XVII

# CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

The Data Governance Framework shall be reviewed through:

- Internal audits.
- Quality assessments.
- Technology upgrades.
- User feedback.
- Governance reviews.

Lessons learned shall inform future improvements.

---

# ARTICLE XVIII

# FUTURE DATA GOVERNANCE VISION

By 2035–2100, TJF shall maintain a world-class enterprise data environment recognized for:

- Trusted information.
- Ethical data use.
- Intelligent analytics.
- Secure digital operations.
- Evidence-based leadership.

---

# FINAL DECLARATION

> "Trusted data is the foundation of trusted decisions. Through responsible governance, The Jopetnah Foundation transforms information into lasting impact."
