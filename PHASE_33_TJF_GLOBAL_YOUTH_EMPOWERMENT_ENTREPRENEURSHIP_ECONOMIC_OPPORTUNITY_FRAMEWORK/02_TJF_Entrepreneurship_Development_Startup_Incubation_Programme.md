# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# ENTREPRENEURSHIP DEVELOPMENT AND STARTUP INCUBATION PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

Entrepreneurship is a powerful driver of innovation, employment creation and economic transformation.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that many young entrepreneurs possess creative ideas and ambition but lack access to business knowledge, mentorship, networks, technology and financial resources required to transform ideas into successful enterprises.

Through this Programme, TJF commits to developing entrepreneurship ecosystems that enable young people to create sustainable businesses, generate employment opportunities and contribute to community prosperity.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Promote entrepreneurship among young people.
- Develop startup creation capacity.
- Provide business incubation support.
- Strengthen innovation ecosystems.
- Connect entrepreneurs with opportunities.
- Support sustainable enterprise growth.

---

# ARTICLE II

# ENTREPRENEURSHIP VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To build a global ecosystem where young entrepreneurs have the knowledge, resources and support required to transform ideas into successful enterprises."

TJF shall:
