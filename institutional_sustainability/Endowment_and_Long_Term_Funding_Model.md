# Endowment and Long-Term Funding Model

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that creating lasting impact requires financial mechanisms capable of supporting institutional priorities beyond annual funding cycles.

An institutional endowment provides a permanent financial foundation by preserving capital, generating sustainable returns and supporting TJF’s mission over generations.

This Endowment and Long-Term Funding Model establishes the governance, investment principles and strategic approach required to build TJF’s long-term financial independence.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Model aims to:

- Establish TJF’s permanent financial sustainability mechanism.
- Protect institutional capital.
- Generate long-term funding capacity.
- Support future programmes and initiatives.
- Strengthen organizational independence.

---

# 2. Endowment Vision

To establish a globally respected institutional endowment that protects TJF’s mission and enables sustainable transformation for generations.

---

# 3. Endowment Mission

To responsibly preserve and grow TJF’s financial assets while generating sustainable resources that advance the Foundation’s strategic objectives.
