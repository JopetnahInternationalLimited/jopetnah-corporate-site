# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# PROGRAMME MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

---

# PREAMBLE

The Jopetnah Foundation delivers its mission through carefully designed programmes that address social, economic, educational, technological and community development needs.

This Programme Management System establishes the standards required to ensure that TJF programmes are:

- Relevant.
- Effective.
- Sustainable.
- Inclusive.
- Measurable.
- Impact-driven.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE OF THE PROGRAMME MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

## Section 1: Objectives

This System aims to:

- Standardize programme development.
- Improve programme delivery.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Measure impact.
- Promote continuous learning.

---

# ARTICLE II

# PROGRAMME MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

All TJF programmes shall follow:

## 1. Community-Centered Approach

Programmes shall respond to genuine community needs.

---

## 2. Evidence-Based Design

Programmes shall be developed using:

- Research.
- Data.
- Community consultation.
- Stakeholder analysis.

---

## 3. Sustainability Principle

Programmes shall consider:

- Long-term benefits.
- Local ownership.
- Resource sustainability.

---

## 4. Impact Orientation

Success shall be measured by meaningful outcomes rather than activities alone.

---

# ARTICLE III

# TJF PROGRAMME LIFECYCLE MODEL

All programmes shall follow:

```
Needs Assessment

        ↓

Programme Design

        ↓

Approval Process

        ↓

Resource Mobilisation

        ↓

Implementation

        ↓

Monitoring

        ↓

Evaluation

        ↓

Learning and Improvement
```

---

# ARTICLE IV

# PROGRAMME IDENTIFICATION AND DESIGN

Before establishing a programme, TJF shall conduct:

## Needs Assessment

Including:

- Community needs.
- Existing challenges.
- Available resources.
- Potential solutions.

---

## Stakeholder Analysis

Identifying:

- Beneficiaries.
- Partners.
- Government stakeholders.
- Community leaders.

---

## Feasibility Assessment

Reviewing:

- Cost.
- Impact potential.
- Risks.
- Sustainability.

---

# ARTICLE V

# PROGRAMME DESIGN FRAMEWORK

Every TJF programme shall define:

## 1. Programme Purpose

The reason the programme exists.

---

## 2. Objectives

Specific outcomes to achieve.

---

## 3. Target Beneficiaries

Groups and communities served.

---

## 4. Activities

Actions required to achieve results.

---

## 5. Resources

Including:

- Budget.
- Staff.
- Equipment.
- Partnerships.

---

## 6. Expected Results

Including:

- Outputs.
- Outcomes.
- Long-term impact.

---

```
Programme Concept

        ↓

Technical Review

        ↓

Budget Assessment

        ↓

Risk Assessment

        ↓

Leadership Approval

        ↓

Implementation Authorization
```

Including:

- Activities.
- Timelines.
- Responsibilities.
- Resources.

---

## Programme Teams

Including:

- Programme Manager.

- Support Personnel.

---

## Operational Procedures

Including:

- Procurement.
- Reporting.
- Compliance requirements.

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# BENEFICIARY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

TJF shall ensure:

- Fair access.
- Respectful engagement.
- Protection of beneficiary dignity.
- Transparent selection processes.

Beneficiary information shall be managed responsibly.

---

# ARTICLE IX

# PROGRAMME MONITORING SYSTEM

Monitoring shall track:

## Activity Progress

Are planned activities being completed?

---

## Resource Utilisation

Are resources being used responsibly?

---

## Performance Indicators

Are expected results being achieved?

---

# ARTICLE X

# PROGRAMME EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

Evaluations shall assess:

## Relevance

Does the programme address real needs?

---

## Effectiveness

Did the programme achieve objectives?

---

## Efficiency

Were resources used effectively?

---

## Sustainability

Can benefits continue beyond programme support?

---

## Impact

What lasting change occurred?

---

# ARTICLE XI

# PROGRAMME PERFORMANCE INDICATORS

TJF programmes shall use:

## Output Indicators

Examples:

- Number of beneficiaries reached.
- Activities completed.
- Services delivered.

---

## Outcome Indicators

Examples:

- Skills improved.
- Opportunities created.
- Living conditions improved.

---

## Impact Indicators

Examples:

- Long-term community transformation.
- Economic empowerment.
- Sustainable development outcomes.

---

# ARTICLE XII

# PROGRAMME RISK MANAGEMENT

Programme risks shall include:

| Risk Area | Management Approach |
|---|---|
| Funding challenges | Resource planning |
| Implementation delays | Project monitoring |
| Community resistance | Engagement strategies |
| Operational failures | Quality controls |
| Sustainability risks | Long-term planning |

---

## Quarterly Reports

- Budget utilisation.
- Results achieved.

---

## Annual Impact Reports

Covering:

- Outcomes.
- Lessons learned.
- Future improvements.

# ARTICLE XIV

# ARTICLE XV

# PROGRAMME KNOWLEDGE MANAGEMENT

- Lessons learned.

- Knowledge documentation.

---

# ARTICLE XVII

# PROGRAMME EXCELLENCE COMMITMENT

Every TJF programme shall strive to:

- Transform lives.
- Strengthen communities.
- Develop opportunities.
- Create lasting impact.
