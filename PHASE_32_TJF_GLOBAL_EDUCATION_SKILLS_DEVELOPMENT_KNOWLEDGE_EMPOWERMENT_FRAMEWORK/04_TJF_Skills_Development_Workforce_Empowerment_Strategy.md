# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# SKILLS DEVELOPMENT AND WORKFORCE EMPOWERMENT STRATEGY

---

# PREAMBLE

The global economy is experiencing rapid transformation driven by technology, innovation, changing industries and evolving workforce requirements.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that access to relevant skills is essential for individuals to secure meaningful employment, create enterprises and contribute to economic development.

Through this Strategy, TJF commits to developing practical skills, strengthening workforce capabilities and empowering individuals to participate successfully in future economies.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Strategy shall:

- Develop future-ready skills.
- Improve employability opportunities.
- Promote entrepreneurship.
- Support technical and vocational education.
- Strengthen workforce competitiveness.
- Encourage lifelong professional development.

---

# ARTICLE II

# WORKFORCE EMPOWERMENT VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To build a globally skilled and empowered workforce capable of creating value, driving innovation and shaping sustainable economic growth."

---

# ARTICLE III

# STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES

TJF shall:

- Identify emerging skills needs.
- Provide accessible training opportunities.
- Support employment pathways.
- Encourage entrepreneurship.
- Build partnerships with industries.
- Promote continuous learning.
