# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# GLOBAL INFORMATION MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

---

# PREAMBLE

The Jopetnah Foundation recognises that information is a strategic institutional resource that enables transparency, accountability, innovation and sustainable development.

This Global Information Management System establishes a standardized approach for managing information across all TJF offices, programmes and partnerships.

The System supports:

- Good governance.
- Operational excellence.
- Institutional memory.
- Regulatory compliance.
- Global collaboration.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

## Objectives

This System shall:

- Standardize information management practices.
- Protect institutional knowledge.
- Improve access to reliable information.
- Support informed decision-making.
- Strengthen operational efficiency.
- Preserve organisational history.

---

# ARTICLE II

# INFORMATION MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

TJF shall manage information according to the following principles:

## Accuracy

Information shall be complete, reliable and verified.

---

## Accessibility

Authorized users shall have timely access to required information.

---

## Confidentiality

Sensitive information shall only be accessed by authorized personnel.

---

## Integrity

Information shall remain accurate and protected from unauthorized alteration.

---

## Availability

Critical information shall remain available whenever required.

---

## Accountability

Every employee shall be responsible for the information under their custody.

# ARTICLE IV

# INFORMATION CLASSIFICATION

All institutional information shall be classified as:

## Public Information

Information approved for public release.

Examples:

- Annual reports.
- Publications.
- Public announcements.
- Educational materials.

---

## Internal Information

Information intended for internal operational use.

Examples:

- Internal procedures.
- Staff communications.
- Operational manuals.

---

## Confidential Information

Information requiring controlled access.

Examples:

- Personnel records.
- Financial reports.
- Donor agreements.
- Procurement documentation.

---

## Highly Restricted Information

Information requiring the highest level of protection.

Examples:

- Cybersecurity credentials.
- Legal investigations.
- Executive deliberations.
- Sensitive beneficiary data.

# ARTICLE VI

# DIGITAL RECORDS MANAGEMENT

TJF shall maintain electronic records for:

- Governance.
- Finance.
- Human Resources.
- Programmes.
- Procurement.
- Partnerships.
- Monitoring and Evaluation.
- Legal Affairs.
- Communications.

Digital records shall remain authentic, searchable and protected.

---

# ARTICLE VII

# DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

The Foundation shall establish an enterprise document management platform supporting:

- Version control.
- Metadata management.
- Electronic approvals.
- Workflow automation.
- Full-text search.
- Audit trails.

---

# ARTICLE VIII

# KNOWLEDGE MANAGEMENT

TJF shall preserve institutional knowledge through:

- Knowledge repositories.
- Lessons learned databases.
- Research libraries.
- Best practice collections.
- Innovation documentation.
- Programme case studies.

Knowledge shall be shared across all departments and country offices.

---

# ARTICLE IX

# INFORMATION SHARING FRAMEWORK

Information sharing shall occur based on:

## Need-to-Know Principle

Users shall only access information necessary for their responsibilities.

---

## Role-Based Access

Access rights shall correspond to assigned institutional roles.

---

## Secure Collaboration

Information exchange shall occur through approved digital platforms.

---

# ARTICLE X

# DATA QUALITY MANAGEMENT

Information quality shall be maintained through:

- Validation procedures.
- Periodic reviews.
- Duplicate detection.
- Standardized templates.
- Metadata standards.

---

# ARTICLE XI

# ARCHIVING AND RETENTION

Information shall be retained according to approved schedules.

Examples:

| Information Type | Minimum Retention |
|------------------|------------------:|
| Governance records | Permanent |
| Financial records | 10 years |
| Human Resource records | 10 years after separation |
| Programme records | 10 years after closure |
| Procurement records | 10 years |
| Research publications | Permanent |

Where national law requires longer retention periods, TJF shall comply with those legal requirements.

---

# ARTICLE XII

# INFORMATION SECURITY

Information shall be protected through:

- Encryption.
- Access controls.
- Secure backups.
- Multi-factor authentication.
- Security monitoring.
- Disaster recovery planning.

---

# ARTICLE XIII

# INFORMATION ACCESS REQUESTS

Authorized personnel may request access through approved procedures.

Requests shall consider:

- Business justification.
- Information classification.
- Legal obligations.
- Privacy requirements.

---

# ARTICLE XIV

# INFORMATION COMPLIANCE

All information management activities shall comply with:

- Applicable national laws.
- Donor requirements.
- Contractual obligations.
- TJF policies.
- Ethical standards.

---

# ARTICLE XV

# DIGITAL COLLABORATION

TJF shall support secure collaboration through:

- Enterprise email.
- Shared workspaces.
- Video conferencing.
- Team collaboration platforms.
- Digital knowledge portals.

---

# ARTICLE XVI

# INFORMATION AUDITS

Periodic audits shall evaluate:

- Information quality.
- Security controls.
- Compliance.
- Retention practices.
- Accessibility.

Recommendations shall support continuous improvement.

---

# ARTICLE XVII

# CAPACITY BUILDING

The Foundation shall provide continuous training on:

- Records management.
- Information governance.
- Digital literacy.
- Information security.
- Knowledge management.

---

# ARTICLE XVIII

# FUTURE INFORMATION MANAGEMENT VISION

By 2035–2100, TJF shall operate a globally integrated information ecosystem that:

- Preserves institutional memory.
- Supports intelligent decision-making.
- Enables secure global collaboration.
- Protects digital assets.
- Accelerates sustainable development.

---

# FINAL DECLARATION

> "Information becomes institutional wisdom when it is managed with integrity, protected with diligence and shared with purpose."
