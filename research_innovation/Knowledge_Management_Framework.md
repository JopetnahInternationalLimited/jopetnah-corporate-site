# Knowledge Management Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes knowledge as a strategic asset essential for organizational effectiveness, innovation, learning and sustainable impact.

Development organizations generate valuable knowledge through research, programme implementation, partnerships, community engagement and operational experience. Without effective systems for capturing, organizing and sharing this knowledge, valuable lessons may be lost.

This Knowledge Management Framework establishes TJF’s institutional approach for collecting, preserving, sharing and applying knowledge to strengthen decision-making, improve programmes and contribute to global development learning.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s knowledge management system.
- Promote organizational learning.
- Capture institutional experiences.
- Improve knowledge sharing.
- Strengthen evidence-based decision-making.
- Preserve organizational memory.

---

This Framework applies to:
