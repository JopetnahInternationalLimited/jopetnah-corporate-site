# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# PHARMACEUTICAL ACCESS PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

Access to safe and effective medicines is a fundamental component of quality healthcare.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that many communities face challenges including medicine shortages, affordability barriers, weak supply chains and limited access to essential healthcare products.

Through this Programme, TJF commits to strengthening pharmaceutical access systems while promoting quality assurance, responsible distribution and sustainable healthcare supply networks.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Improve access to essential medicines.
- Strengthen pharmaceutical supply chains.
- Promote medicine quality and safety.
- Support vulnerable communities.
- Encourage responsible pharmaceutical partnerships.
- Improve healthcare resilience.

---

# ARTICLE II

# PHARMACEUTICAL ACCESS VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To create a future where every community has reliable access to safe, affordable and quality-assured medicines and healthcare products."

---

# ARTICLE III

# PROGRAMME PRINCIPLES

TJF pharmaceutical initiatives shall follow:

## 1. Quality First

All healthcare products must meet appropriate quality and safety requirements.

---

## 2. Accessibility

Medicines should reach communities that need them most.

---

## 3. Affordability

Solutions should consider economic barriers faced by vulnerable populations.

---

## 4. Safety

Pharmaceutical handling must protect patients and communities.

---

## 5. Transparency

Supply chains must operate responsibly and ethically.

---

## 6. Sustainability

Healthcare supply systems must remain effective over time.
