# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# WATER INFRASTRUCTURE AND ENGINEERING DEVELOPMENT FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

Reliable water infrastructure is essential for sustainable development, public health, agriculture, industry and community resilience.

Many communities face water challenges due to inadequate infrastructure, aging systems, limited investment and insufficient maintenance capacity.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that effective water infrastructure requires integrated engineering approaches combining innovation, sustainability, affordability and long-term operational planning.

Through this Framework, TJF commits to supporting the development of resilient water infrastructure systems that improve access, strengthen communities and protect future water resources.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Framework shall:

- Promote sustainable water infrastructure development.
- Support engineering-based water solutions.
- Improve water supply reliability.
- Encourage infrastructure innovation.
