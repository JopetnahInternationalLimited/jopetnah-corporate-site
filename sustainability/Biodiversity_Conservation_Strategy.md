# Biodiversity Conservation Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes biodiversity as a critical foundation for ecological balance, human wellbeing, economic development and planetary resilience.

Biodiversity — including plants, animals, microorganisms, ecosystems and genetic resources — provides essential services such as food security, clean water, climate regulation, medicine, livelihoods and cultural value.

However, biodiversity faces increasing threats from habitat destruction, climate change, pollution, unsustainable resource use and human activities. TJF therefore commits to supporting biodiversity protection, ecosystem restoration and community-led conservation initiatives.

This Biodiversity Conservation Strategy establishes TJF’s commitment to protecting nature while promoting sustainable development and community prosperity.
