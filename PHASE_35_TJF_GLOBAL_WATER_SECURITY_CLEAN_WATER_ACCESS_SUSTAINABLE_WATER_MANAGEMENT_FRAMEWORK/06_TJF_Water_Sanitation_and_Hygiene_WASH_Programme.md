# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# WATER, SANITATION AND HYGIENE (WASH) PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

Safe water, sanitation and hygiene are fundamental pillars of healthy and prosperous societies.

Communities without adequate sanitation and hygiene systems face increased risks of disease, reduced educational participation, lower productivity and weakened social development.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes WASH as a critical component of sustainable development and community transformation.

Through this Programme, TJF commits to supporting integrated WASH solutions that improve health, protect dignity and create healthier communities worldwide.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Expand access to safe sanitation systems.
- Promote hygiene education.
- Improve community health outcomes.
- Support schools and healthcare facilities.
- Strengthen WASH infrastructure.
- Encourage sustainable sanitation practices.
