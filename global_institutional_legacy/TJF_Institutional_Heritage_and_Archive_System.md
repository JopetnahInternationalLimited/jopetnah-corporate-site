# TJF Institutional Heritage and Archive System

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes institutional memory as a valuable legacy asset.

The experiences, achievements, decisions, innovations and knowledge accumulated throughout TJF’s journey represent a foundation for future growth and learning.

This Institutional Heritage and Archive System establishes the structures required to preserve TJF’s history and ensure that future generations can access, understand and build upon the Foundation’s legacy.

---

# 1. Purpose

This System aims to:

- Preserve TJF institutional history.
- Protect important records and knowledge.
- Document achievements and milestones.
- Support future learning and leadership.
- Establish a permanent institutional memory system.

---

# 2. Heritage Vision

To create a world-class institutional heritage system that preserves TJF’s story, knowledge and contributions for generations.

---

# 3. Heritage Mission

To collect, protect, organize and share TJF’s historical and intellectual assets to strengthen institutional identity and future transformation.

---

# 4. Heritage Philosophy

TJF believes:

> "An institution that remembers its journey can better design its future."

Institutional heritage requires:

- Preservation.
- Documentation.
- Accessibility.
- Accuracy.
- Continuity.
