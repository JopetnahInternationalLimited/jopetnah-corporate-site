# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# GLOBAL FOOD SECURITY STRATEGY

---

# PREAMBLE

Food security is one of humanity's most important challenges.

A secure food system ensures that individuals and communities have reliable access to sufficient, safe and nutritious food while protecting the environment and strengthening economic opportunities.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that achieving sustainable food security requires collaboration among farmers, communities, governments, businesses, researchers, technology innovators and development partners.

Through this Strategy, TJF commits to supporting resilient food systems that empower producers, strengthen agricultural economies and improve community wellbeing.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Strategy shall:

- Establish TJF's global food security direction.
- Support sustainable agricultural development.
- Strengthen food production systems.
- Promote agricultural innovation.
- Improve nutrition and food access.
- Empower farming communities.
