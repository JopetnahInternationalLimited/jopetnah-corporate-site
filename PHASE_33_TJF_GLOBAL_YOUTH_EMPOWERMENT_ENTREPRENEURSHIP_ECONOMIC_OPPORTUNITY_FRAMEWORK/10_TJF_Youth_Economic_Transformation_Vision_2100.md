# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# YOUTH ECONOMIC TRANSFORMATION VISION 2100

---

# PREAMBLE

Young people represent the creativity, energy and innovation capacity required to shape the future of humanity.

However, sustainable economic transformation requires systems that enable young people to access education, skills, capital, technology, markets and leadership opportunities.

The Jopetnah Foundation believes that youth should not only participate in economies but actively shape them through entrepreneurship, innovation, professional excellence and responsible leadership.

This Vision establishes TJF's commitment to supporting generations of young people from 2026 to 2100.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Vision shall:

- Define TJF's long-term youth economic ambition.
- Guide future youth empowerment programmes.
- Promote entrepreneurship and innovation.
- Support inclusive economic participation.
- Create pathways toward sustainable prosperity.

---

# ARTICLE II

# YOUTH ECONOMIC TRANSFORMATION VISION

## Vision Statement

> "By the year 2100, The Jopetnah Foundation envisions a world where every young person has the opportunity, capability and resources to create value, build sustainable livelihoods and contribute meaningfully to global economic progress."
