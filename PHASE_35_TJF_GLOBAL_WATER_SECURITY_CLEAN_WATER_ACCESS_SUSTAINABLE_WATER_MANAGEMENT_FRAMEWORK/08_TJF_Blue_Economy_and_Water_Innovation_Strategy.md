# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# BLUE ECONOMY AND WATER INNOVATION STRATEGY

---

# PREAMBLE

Water ecosystems provide essential environmental, social and economic benefits to humanity.

Marine and freshwater resources support livelihoods, food systems, transportation, biodiversity, tourism, research and industrial development.

However, increasing environmental pressures, pollution, climate change and unsustainable exploitation threaten the long-term sustainability of aquatic resources.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes the blue economy as an opportunity to combine economic growth with environmental responsibility.

Through this Strategy, TJF commits to supporting innovative water solutions, sustainable aquatic development and responsible use of water resources for present and future generations.
