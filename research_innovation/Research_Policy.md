# Research Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes research as a fundamental driver of evidence-based decision-making, sustainable development and social transformation.

Effective development solutions require reliable data, rigorous analysis and continuous learning. TJF therefore commits to conducting, supporting and sharing high-quality research that addresses critical social, economic, environmental and humanitarian challenges.

This Research Policy establishes TJF’s institutional framework for generating knowledge, promoting innovation, strengthening evidence-based programming and contributing to global development dialogue.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s research principles and standards.
- Promote evidence-based programming.
- Support innovative solutions.
- Strengthen institutional learning.
- Encourage knowledge sharing.
- Contribute to global development research.
