# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# GLOBAL YOUTH ECONOMIC PARTNERSHIP NETWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

Economic transformation is achieved through cooperation, shared knowledge and collective action.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that young entrepreneurs and professionals require access to strong networks that connect them with opportunities, expertise, technology, finance and global markets.

Through this Network, TJF commits to developing a worldwide partnership ecosystem that enables young people to participate effectively in local, regional and global economies.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Network shall:

- Establish global youth economic partnerships.
- Connect young entrepreneurs with opportunities.
- Promote knowledge exchange.
- Strengthen entrepreneurship ecosystems.
- Mobilize resources for youth development.
- Encourage international collaboration.

---

# ARTICLE II

# PARTNERSHIP VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To build a connected global ecosystem where young people collaborate, innovate and access opportunities that accelerate economic progress."

---

# ARTICLE III

# STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES

TJF shall:

- Develop strategic partnerships worldwide.
- Create youth business networks.
- Connect entrepreneurs with investors.
- Encourage international trade opportunities.
- Promote innovation collaboration.
- Strengthen economic inclusion.
