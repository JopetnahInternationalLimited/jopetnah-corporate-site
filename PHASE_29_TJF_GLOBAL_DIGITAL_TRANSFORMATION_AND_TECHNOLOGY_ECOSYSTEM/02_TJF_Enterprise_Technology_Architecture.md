# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# ENTERPRISE TECHNOLOGY ARCHITECTURE

---

# PREAMBLE

The Jopetnah Foundation requires a modern technology ecosystem capable of supporting its growing global presence, diverse programmes and complex operational requirements.

The Enterprise Technology Architecture provides the structural foundation for:

- Reliable digital services.
- Integrated systems.
- Secure information management.
- Global collaboration.
- Future innovation.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE OF THE ENTERPRISE TECHNOLOGY ARCHITECTURE

## Objectives

This Architecture aims to:

- Establish a unified technology blueprint.
- Enable scalable digital growth.
- Reduce technology fragmentation.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Support innovation.

---

# ARTICLE II

# TJF TECHNOLOGY VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To build a secure, intelligent and globally integrated technology ecosystem that enables TJF to deliver greater impact through innovation and digital excellence."

---

# ARTICLE III

# ENTERPRISE ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLES

TJF technology development shall follow:

---

# 1. Strategic Alignment

Technology investments must support TJF’s mission and strategic objectives.

---

# 2. Integration

Systems shall communicate effectively and avoid isolated solutions.

---

# 3. Scalability

Technology shall support growth across:

- Countries.
- Regions.
- Programmes.
- Departments.

---

# 4. Security

Security shall be embedded into every technology decision.

---

# 5. User-Centred Design

Systems shall be designed around the needs of:

- Employees.
- Partners.
- Communities.
- Beneficiaries.

---
