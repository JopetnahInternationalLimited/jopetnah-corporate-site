# Revenue Generation Model

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable impact requires diversified and reliable sources of income.

While grants and donations remain important, long-term institutional strength requires the development of ethical revenue-generating activities that support programme delivery, organizational growth and financial independence.

This Revenue Generation Model establishes TJF’s framework for developing, managing and expanding sustainable income streams that align with the Foundation’s mission and create measurable social value.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Model aims to:

- Establish TJF’s revenue generation framework.
- Create sustainable income sources.
- Reduce dependency on external funding.
- Support programme expansion.
- Strengthen financial resilience.
- Promote mission-aligned enterprise development.

---

# 2. Scope

This Model applies to:

- Earned income activities.
- Consultancy services.
- Training programmes.
- Knowledge products.
- Social enterprises.
- Commercial partnerships.
- Investment-related income.
- Strategic ventures.

---

# 3. Revenue Generation Vision

To build a diversified and sustainable income ecosystem that enables TJF to expand its social impact while maintaining financial independence.

---

# 4. Revenue Generation Mission

To develop innovative, ethical and scalable income models that convert expertise, resources and opportunities into sustainable funding for community transformation.

---

# 5. Revenue Generation Principles

TJF revenue activities shall follow:

## Mission Alignment

All income activities must support TJF’s purpose.

---

## Ethical Business Practices

Revenue generation must maintain integrity and responsibility.

---

## Sustainability

Activities should create long-term financial value.

---

## Innovation

TJF shall explore new and creative income opportunities.

---

## Transparency

Revenue sources and utilization shall be properly reported.

---

## Impact Reinvestment

A portion of generated income shall support social programmes.

---

TJF seeks to:
