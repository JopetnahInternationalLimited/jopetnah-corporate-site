# International Partnership Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable development requires collaboration among governments, international organizations, civil society, private sector actors, academic institutions and communities.

Global challenges such as poverty, climate change, healthcare inequality, education gaps, economic vulnerability and humanitarian crises cannot be solved by one institution alone. Strategic partnerships enable organizations to combine expertise, resources, innovation and networks to create greater impact.

This International Partnership Strategy establishes TJF’s framework for identifying, developing, managing and sustaining meaningful partnerships that advance its mission and global development objectives.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s global partnership approach.
- Build strategic institutional relationships.
- Expand international collaboration.
- Mobilize resources and expertise.
- Strengthen programme impact.
- Increase TJF’s global presence.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to partnerships with:

- Governments.
- International organizations.
- United Nations agencies.
- NGOs.
- Civil society organizations.
- Universities.
- Research institutions.
- Corporations.
- Philanthropic organizations.
- Development networks.

---

# 3. Partnership Vision

To establish TJF as a trusted global development partner connecting institutions, resources and ideas to create sustainable impact.

---

# 4. Partnership Mission

To build strategic, transparent and mutually beneficial partnerships that strengthen TJF’s ability to improve lives and contribute to sustainable development.

---

# 5. Partnership Principles

All TJF partnerships shall follow:

## Shared Purpose

Partnerships must align with common development objectives.

---

## Mutual Benefit

Each partner should contribute and receive meaningful value.

---

## Transparency

Partnerships shall operate openly and responsibly.

---

## Accountability

Roles, commitments and expectations must be clearly defined.

---

## Respect

Partners shall recognize diverse expertise, cultures and perspectives.

---

## Sustainability

Partnerships should create long-term value beyond individual projects.

---
