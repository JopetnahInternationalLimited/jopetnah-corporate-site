# Climate Action Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes climate change as one of the defining challenges affecting communities, ecosystems, economies and sustainable development worldwide.

Climate-related challenges including extreme weather events, food insecurity, water stress, biodiversity loss and displacement increasingly affect vulnerable populations. TJF therefore commits to supporting climate solutions that combine environmental protection, community resilience, sustainable livelihoods and innovative approaches.

This Climate Action Strategy establishes TJF’s institutional roadmap for reducing environmental impacts, supporting climate adaptation and contributing to global climate action efforts.

---

This Strategy aims to:

- Define TJF’s climate action priorities.
- Integrate climate considerations into programmes.
- Support climate-resilient communities.
- Reduce institutional carbon footprint.
- Promote climate innovation.
- Strengthen partnerships for climate solutions.
