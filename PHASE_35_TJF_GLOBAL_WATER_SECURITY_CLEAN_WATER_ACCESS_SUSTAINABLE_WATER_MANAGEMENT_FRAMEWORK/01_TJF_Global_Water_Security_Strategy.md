# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# GLOBAL WATER SECURITY STRATEGY

---

# PREAMBLE

Water is one of the most essential resources for human survival, economic prosperity and environmental balance.

Across the world, communities continue to experience challenges including limited access to safe drinking water, inadequate water infrastructure, climate-related water stress, pollution and unsustainable resource management.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that sustainable water security requires a comprehensive approach combining infrastructure development, innovation, conservation, community participation and global cooperation.

Through this Strategy, TJF commits to supporting water systems that improve lives, protect ecosystems and strengthen resilience for present and future generations.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Strategy shall:

- Establish TJF’s global water security direction.
- Promote universal access to safe water.
- Support sustainable water resource management.
- Encourage water infrastructure development.

- Promote water innovation and partnerships.
