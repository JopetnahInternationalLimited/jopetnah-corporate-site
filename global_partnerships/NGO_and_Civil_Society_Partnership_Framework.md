# NGO and Civil Society Partnership Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that strong civil society partnerships are essential for achieving sustainable development and creating meaningful community impact.

Non-governmental organizations (NGOs), community-based organizations, advocacy groups and grassroots movements play a critical role in understanding local challenges, mobilizing communities and delivering solutions.

This NGO and Civil Society Partnership Framework establishes TJF’s approach for building effective, ethical and sustainable collaborations with civil society actors at local, national, regional and global levels.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s civil society partnership approach.
- Strengthen community-based collaboration.
- Support collective impact initiatives.
- Expand development networks.
- Improve programme effectiveness.
- Promote knowledge and resource sharing.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to partnerships with:

- Local NGOs.
- International NGOs.
- Community-based organizations.
- Faith-based organizations.
- Advocacy networks.
- Youth organizations.
- Women’s organizations.
- Human rights organizations.
- Grassroots movements.

---

# 3. Partnership Vision

To build a global network of civil society partnerships that empowers communities and accelerates sustainable development.

---

# 4. Partnership Mission

To collaborate with civil society organizations through shared knowledge, resources and expertise to create inclusive and lasting solutions.
