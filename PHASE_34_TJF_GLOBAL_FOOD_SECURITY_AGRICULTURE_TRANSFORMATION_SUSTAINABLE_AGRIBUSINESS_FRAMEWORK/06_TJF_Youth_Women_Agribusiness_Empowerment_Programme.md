# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# YOUTH AND WOMEN AGRIBUSINESS EMPOWERMENT PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

Agriculture represents one of the greatest opportunities for youth employment, women empowerment and rural economic transformation.

Despite their significant contribution to agricultural production, many young people and women continue to face barriers including limited access to finance, technology, markets, training and business networks.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that inclusive agricultural development requires deliberate investment in youth and women as farmers, entrepreneurs, innovators and leaders.

Through this Programme, TJF commits to creating opportunities that enable young people and women to participate fully in modern agricultural economies.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Promote youth participation in agriculture.
- Strengthen women-led agribusinesses.
- Create agricultural employment opportunities.
- Support inclusive enterprise development.
- Increase access to agricultural resources.
- Build future agricultural leaders.
