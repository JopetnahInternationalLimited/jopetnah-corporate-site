Company Overview

About Jopetnah International Limited

A Kampala-based multi-sector services company established in 2016, empowering businesses and organisations with practical, integrated solutions across 16 strategic sectors.

Jopetnah International Limited is a Kampala-based multi-sector services company established in 2016, providing practical and integrated solutions across business, professional, technical, and development-oriented areas.

Our capabilities span 16 strategic sectors, bringing together complementary expertise to help businesses, institutions, development partners, investors, and communities address challenges, strengthen capabilities, pursue sustainable opportunities, and create measurable value.

Rather than operating as a collection of unrelated services, Jopetnah seeks to connect complementary capabilities around the needs of each engagement. This enables clients to access coordinated support while reducing the complexity of managing multiple separate providers.

Where specialised expertise, resources, licensing, or geographic reach is required beyond our direct capabilities, we work with appropriate professional partners and collaborators while remaining transparent about the scope of our role.

How We Started

Our Story

Founded in Kampala with an initial focus on consultancy and capacity development, Jopetnah has progressively expanded its service offering in response to evolving client needs and emerging opportunities.

Over time, the organisation has developed capabilities across multiple sectors while building relationships with professionals, businesses, institutions, suppliers, collaborators, and strategic partners in Uganda and beyond.

Today, Jopetnah combines its Ugandan foundation with an expanding international business network. Our approach remains deliberately practical:

Understand the need → bring together the right capabilities → work with appropriate partners where necessary → focus on responsible delivery.

Our growth is guided not simply by the number of sectors or markets in which we operate, but by the quality, relevance, and sustainability of the solutions we provide.

We believe sustainable growth is built on delivery excellence — not on rapid expansion claims.

Our Vision

To be a trusted Ugandan partner for practical, integrated business and development solutions that create measurable value for our clients and the communities we serve.

Our Mission

To deliver professional, results-oriented services that help organisations improve operations, build capacity, and pursue sustainable growth — through integrity, collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement.

Our Approach

How We Work

Our delivery approach is built around practicality, coordination, professionalism, and accountability.

01. Understand

We begin by understanding the client's objectives, operating environment, challenges, resources, and desired outcomes.

02. Define

We clarify the scope of work, responsibilities, priorities, deliverables, and appropriate approach.

03. Integrate

Where an engagement requires multiple capabilities, we coordinate complementary expertise around a common objective.

04. Deliver

We focus on practical implementation, clear communication, professional standards, and responsible execution.

05. Improve

We learn from experience, seek feedback, strengthen our systems, and continuously improve our service delivery.

This approach is intended to provide clients with clearer communication, reduced coordination overhead, and practical implementation support. We remain transparent about the scope of our current capabilities and the role of partners in extending our reach.

Managing Director

Okello Carlos Torach

Managing Director · Business & Finance Strategist · Operations Executive

Financial stewardship. Strategic insight. Sustainable growth.

Executive Profile

Okello Carlos Torach is the Managing Director of Jopetnah International Limited (JIL), with over 15 years of professional experience spanning NGO, manufacturing and private-sector environments. His professional background combines financial management, organisational leadership, operational coordination and economic policy management.

His leadership philosophy is founded on a clear principle: rigorous financial stewardship fuels visionary strategy. He believes that sustainable organisational performance requires a strong financial foundation, disciplined execution, effective governance and the ability to translate strategic insight into practical action.

At JIL, he provides strategic direction and oversees the alignment of business objectives, operational priorities and resource management. His approach emphasises integrity, accountability, collaboration and continuous improvement, supporting the company's commitment to practical, integrated business solutions.

Bridging Financial Discipline and Strategic Growth

Carlos's academic training combines a Master of Arts in Economic Policy Management from Makerere University (College of Business & Management Science), a Bachelor of Business Administration in Accounting from Makerere University, and a Diploma in Accounting & Finance from Makerere University Business School.

This foundation informs his ability to examine organisational challenges from both financial and economic perspectives. His areas of professional focus include financial planning and reporting, budgeting and forecasting, internal controls, taxation, corporate governance, procurement, inventory management, supply-chain coordination and operational performance.

Leadership Priorities

Strategic Leadership & Organisational Alignment

Translating organisational objectives into practical priorities, strengthening communication and aligning people, processes and resources around shared goals.

Financial Stewardship & Integrity

Promoting disciplined financial management through budgeting, forecasting, financial reporting, working-capital management, internal controls and responsible resource allocation.

Operational Excellence & Efficiency

Identifying opportunities to improve business processes, strengthen procurement and inventory management, coordinate supply chains and improve the use of organisational resources.

Governance, Risk & Sustainability

Supporting accountability, risk awareness, appropriate compliance practices and robust internal controls that protect organisational assets and strengthen resilience.

Digital Transformation & Data-Informed Decision-Making

Applying financial systems, analytical tools, business intelligence and appropriate digital technologies to improve management visibility, operational coordination and decision-making.

Professional Experience

Carlos's career includes leadership and operational responsibilities at Jopetnah International Limited, where he has served as Managing Director since 2017.

Previously, he served as Acting National Coordinator at Radio Maria Uganda Limited, following an earlier tenure as Finance and Administrative Officer at the same organisation. His earlier professional experience includes work in inventory control and logistics management at Makss Packaging Industries Ltd.

These roles provide a professional foundation spanning executive management, finance and administration, organisational coordination, internal controls, logistics and operational oversight.

An Integrated Approach to Business

Carlos's leadership perspective aligns with JIL's commitment to connecting complementary capabilities across its strategic sectors. He champions coordinated solutions that respond to business and institutional needs through practical service delivery, informed decision-making and appropriate specialist partnerships.

For him, sustainable growth is not simply an increase in revenue. It also involves financial discipline, effective execution, responsible governance, adaptability and the capacity to create enduring value for clients and the communities organisations serve.

Professional Outlook

Carlos remains focused on strengthening organisational performance, improving operational effectiveness, supporting responsible digital transformation and building relationships that contribute to sustainable business growth.

His guiding conviction remains consistent: strong financial foundations, informed strategic decisions and disciplined execution are essential to sustainable growth.
