# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# SCHOLARSHIP AND EDUCATION ACCESS PROGRAMME

---

# PREAMBLE

Education has the power to transform lives, families and communities.

However, millions of talented learners continue to experience barriers including financial hardship, limited educational infrastructure, social challenges and unequal access to learning opportunities.

The Jopetnah Foundation is committed to ensuring that capable and motivated individuals are supported to pursue education, develop skills and contribute positively to society.

Through this Programme, TJF will establish sustainable scholarship pathways, education support systems and partnerships that expand learning opportunities worldwide.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Programme shall:

- Increase access to quality education.
- Support deserving learners.
- Reduce education inequality.
- Promote academic excellence.
- Develop future leaders.
- Strengthen community development through education.

---

# ARTICLE II

# SCHOLARSHIP VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To create a future where financial and social barriers do not prevent talented individuals from achieving their educational dreams."

# ARTICLE III

TJF Scholarship and Education Access Programme shall:
