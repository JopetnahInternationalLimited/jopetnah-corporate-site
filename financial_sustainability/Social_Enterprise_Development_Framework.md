# Social Enterprise Development Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes social enterprises as powerful instruments for combining economic sustainability with positive social impact.

Unlike traditional businesses that focus primarily on financial returns, social enterprises pursue a dual objective:

1. Generate sustainable revenue.
2. Address social, economic and environmental challenges.

This Social Enterprise Development Framework establishes TJF’s approach for designing, developing, managing and scaling mission-aligned enterprises that support communities while strengthening the Foundation’s long-term financial sustainability.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s social enterprise model.
- Create sustainable income-generating opportunities.
- Support community empowerment.
- Encourage entrepreneurship.
- Develop financially viable impact ventures.
- Strengthen institutional sustainability.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- TJF-owned social enterprises.
- Community enterprises.
- Impact-driven businesses.
- Social innovation projects.
- Enterprise partnerships.
- Entrepreneurial development programmes.

---

# 3. Social Enterprise Vision

To build sustainable enterprises that transform communities, create opportunities and finance long-term social impact.

---

# 4. Social Enterprise Mission

To develop innovative business models that generate financial value while advancing TJF’s social development objectives.

---

# 5. Social Enterprise Principles

TJF social enterprises shall follow:

## Mission First

Business activities must support TJF’s social purpose.

---

## Financial Sustainability

Enterprises must develop viable revenue models.

---

## Community Empowerment

Priority shall be given to initiatives that improve livelihoods.

---

## Innovation

Enterprises should introduce effective solutions.

---

## Ethical Business

Operations must maintain integrity and responsibility.

---

## Impact Measurement

Social and economic outcomes must be measured.

---

---

## Financial Viability

## Scalability

---

## Strategic Alignment
