# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# SUSTAINABLE WATER MANAGEMENT AND CONSERVATION STRATEGY

---

# PREAMBLE

Water resources are essential for human survival, economic development, agricultural production and environmental sustainability.

Increasing population growth, climate change, pollution, industrial expansion and unsustainable consumption patterns continue to place pressure on global water resources.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes that sustainable water security requires a balanced approach that protects natural water systems while ensuring communities and economies have access to reliable water resources.

Through this Strategy, TJF commits to advancing responsible water management practices that conserve, protect and sustainably utilize water resources.

---
