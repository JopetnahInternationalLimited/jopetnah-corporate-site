# Website and Digital Experience Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes its website as the primary digital gateway connecting communities, donors, partners, governments, volunteers and global audiences with its mission and programmes.

A modern website must function beyond a traditional information platform. It must become an interactive digital ecosystem that communicates impact, enables engagement, supports fundraising, facilitates partnerships and strengthens institutional credibility.

This Website and Digital Experience Strategy establishes TJF’s approach for developing a world-class digital platform that delivers a seamless, accessible and inspiring experience for all stakeholders.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s website as a global digital headquarters.
- Improve stakeholder experience.
- Increase online engagement.
- Support digital fundraising.
- Strengthen institutional visibility.
- Enable digital transformation.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy covers:

- Website architecture.
