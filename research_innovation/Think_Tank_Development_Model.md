# Think Tank Development Model

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes the importance of independent research, policy dialogue and evidence-based advocacy in addressing complex global challenges.

A dedicated think tank function will enable TJF to generate strategic insights, influence development policies, convene experts and contribute to global discussions on sustainable development.

The TJF Think Tank Model establishes the framework for building an institution that combines research excellence, policy engagement, innovation and knowledge leadership.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Model aims to:

- Establish TJF’s think tank development pathway.
- Strengthen policy research capacity.
- Create platforms for expert engagement.
- Influence development discussions.
- Support evidence-based decision-making.
- Build global intellectual partnerships.

---
