# THE JOPETNAH FOUNDATION (TJF)

# CIRCULAR ECONOMY FRAMEWORK

---

# PREAMBLE

The traditional linear economic model of "take, make and dispose" creates significant environmental pressures through resource depletion, waste generation and pollution.

The Jopetnah Foundation recognizes the importance of transitioning toward a circular economy model that keeps resources in productive use, minimizes waste and supports sustainable development.

This Framework establishes TJF's commitment to responsible consumption, resource efficiency and innovative approaches to environmental sustainability.

---

# ARTICLE I

# PURPOSE

This Framework shall:

- Establish TJF's circular economy principles.
- Reduce waste generation.
- Promote resource efficiency.
- Encourage reuse and recycling.
- Support sustainable innovation.
- Strengthen environmental responsibility.

---

# ARTICLE II

# CIRCULAR ECONOMY VISION

## Vision Statement

> "To create a regenerative institutional and community ecosystem where resources are valued, waste is minimized and sustainable solutions create lasting environmental benefits."

---

# ARTICLE III

# CIRCULAR ECONOMY PRINCIPLES

TJF shall promote:

## 1. Reduce

Minimizing unnecessary consumption and resource use.

---

## 2. Reuse

Extending the useful life of materials and equipment.

---

## 3. Repair

Encouraging maintenance and restoration rather than replacement.

---

## 4. Recycle

Recovering materials for continued use.

---

## 5. Recover

Capturing value from materials that cannot be reused.

---

## 6. Regenerate

Supporting solutions that restore natural systems.

The Framework shall operate through six strategic pillars:

TJF shall encourage:

- Responsible purchasing.

- Efficient resource utilization.
- Awareness of environmental impacts.

The Foundation shall prioritize:

- Avoiding waste generation.

- Sustainable materials.

# Pillar 3 — Resource Recovery

TJF shall promote:

- Recycling systems.
- Material recovery.
- Responsible disposal.
- Partnerships with recycling organizations.

- Product durability.
- Repairability.

- Sustainable technologies.

- Recycling initiatives.

---

# ARTICLE VI

# ZERO WASTE APPROACH

TJF shall progressively work toward a zero waste approach by:

- Preventing unnecessary waste.
- Increasing recycling.
- Reducing disposable materials.
- Improving resource planning.
- Encouraging responsible behaviour.

Zero waste shall be pursued through continuous improvement rather than immediate elimination of all waste.
