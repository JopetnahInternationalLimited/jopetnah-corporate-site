# Financial Resilience Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable impact requires not only financial growth but also the ability to withstand economic uncertainty, funding disruptions and operational challenges.

Financial resilience enables an organization to continue serving communities during periods of crisis while protecting institutional stability.

This Financial Resilience Strategy establishes TJF’s approach for maintaining financial strength through prudent planning, risk management, diversified income sources, reserve mechanisms and adaptive financial decision-making.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Strengthen TJF’s financial stability.
- Protect programme continuity.
- Reduce exposure to financial shocks.
- Establish crisis response mechanisms.
- Improve financial planning.
- Build long-term organizational resilience.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to:

# 9. Financial Scenario Planning

TJF shall prepare for:

## Normal Scenario

Conditions:

- Stable funding.
- Planned growth.
- Expected operations.

Response:

- Continue strategic implementation.

---

## Moderate Challenge Scenario

Conditions:

- Reduced income.
- Increased costs.
- Temporary disruption.

Response:

- Adjust spending.
- Activate reserves.
- Prioritize essential programmes.

---

## Severe Crisis Scenario

Conditions:

- Major funding loss.
- Economic disruption.
- Emergency conditions.

Response:

- Activate crisis financial plan.
- Protect critical programmes.
- Mobilize alternative resources.

---

# 10. Emergency Financial Response Framework

During financial emergencies, TJF shall:

1. Assess financial impact.
2. Identify immediate priorities.
3. Protect essential programmes.
4. Review expenditure commitments.
5. Activate available reserves.
6. Seek emergency partnerships.
7. Develop recovery plans.
