# Future Trends and Foresight Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that the future will be shaped by rapid technological change, environmental pressures, demographic shifts, economic transformation and evolving social needs.

Organizations that anticipate change are better positioned to create sustainable impact. TJF therefore commits to developing strategic foresight capabilities that enable proactive decision-making, innovation and long-term planning.

This Future Trends and Foresight Strategy establishes TJF’s approach to understanding emerging trends, developing future scenarios and preparing programmes, partnerships and investments for future opportunities and challenges.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s foresight approach.
- Monitor emerging global trends.
- Support long-term strategic planning.
- Identify future opportunities.
- Prepare for emerging risks.
- Strengthen innovation capacity.
